## Recommended initial customer

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]`

Prioritize an individual independent golf instructor or teaching professional in Canada who personally controls or influences the assessment-to-package recommendation and can adopt simple software without a facility purchasing process.

The initial segment is facility-neutral and Canada-wide. Outdoor club professionals, independent range instructors, indoor/simulator coaches, and solo academy operators can all qualify when the individual instructor controls the relevant workflow.

Small academies and multi-coach facilities are later self-serve team-tier candidates. They are not the initial enterprise-sales beachhead.

## Ideal customer profile

### Required inclusion criteria

A strong initial instructor:

1. personally controls or materially influences how lesson packages are sold;
2. conducts assessments, evaluations, or initial lessons;
3. recommends multiple lessons to at least some golfers;
4. wants more golfers to commit to structured coaching;
5. already has coach observations and at least some optional evidence such as video, drills, measurements, or on-course reports;
6. wants a clearer, more professional golfer experience;
7. wants to preserve current coaching tools and methodology;
8. is comfortable using simple self-serve software;
9. can describe or reconstruct a recent assessment-to-package decision; and
10. could plausibly compare value with **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] approximately CAD $75 per month**, without that comparison being treated as willingness-to-pay proof.

### Strong-fit signals

- Repeated assessments or first-session recommendations across the active season.
- A package or phase-based offer, even if it is currently explained verbally.
- A reputation-led business where clarity, professionalism, and referrals matter.
- Frustration with fragmented notes, videos, or follow-up.
- A desire to sell a defined coaching phase rather than isolated hours.
- Direct access to eligible golfers and the ability to share a roadmap.
- Willingness to test a standardized product and report support needs honestly.

### Not required

The instructor does not need to:

- own a course, range, studio, or academy;
- manage multiple instructors;
- use a particular launch monitor or analysis product;
- follow a proprietary coaching method;
- operate in a specific Canadian city or province;
- replace booking, payment, communication, or coaching systems;
- buy consulting, setup, or custom branding; or
- commit to twelve consecutive paid months.

### Exclusion or boundary criteria for initial research

- One-off lessons are the dominant model and the instructor has no interest in structured packages.
- No assessment or recommendation moment exists.
- Corporate rules prevent the instructor from changing client-facing follow-up.
- The participant wants the product to diagnose swings or replace coaching methodology.
- Facility procurement, multi-coach permissions, or custom implementation is mandatory to participate.
- Junior golfers are required before parent/guardian, safeguarding, privacy, and consent questions are resolved.

Boundary cases may be interviewed to learn why they do not fit; they should not be counted as proof of the core segment.

## Canada-wide discovery sample

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`

Recruit 12 instructors for discovery:

- 10 solo or individually controlled core-segment instructors;
- 2 small-team or facility boundary participants to identify future-tier pressure without steering the first scope;
- a mix of outdoor/seasonal and indoor/year-round instruction;
- representation from more than one Canadian region and no single local-market dependency;
- varied package maturity, from mostly verbal offers to defined programs; and
- enough recent assessment examples to discuss actual behavior rather than general opinion.

This is a qualitative decision sample, not a market-size estimate or national representation claim.

| Role | Initial definition | Decision implication |
|---|---|---|
| Economic buyer | The individual instructor. | Price and terms must be understandable without a proposal or approval chain. |
| Primary user | The same instructor who assesses the golfer and owns the recommendation. | Setup, authoring, preview, share, and return must work without an implementation specialist. |

## Recommended initial golfer

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`

Begin prototype and discovery work with an adult recreational golfer who:

- has a personally meaningful scoring or performance goal;
- recently completed an assessment or initial coaching session;
- is considering a multi-session recommendation;
- has enough motivation to practise or play;
- is uncertain whether continued coaching justifies time and money; and
- wants a clear plan rather than a technical data dump.

The existing synthetic mid-handicap golfer seeking to break 90 remains suitable for consistency testing, but synthetic content is never participant evidence.

## Seasonality profiles to include

`[UNVALIDATED BUSINESS ASSUMPTION]` Canadian instructors may have materially different willingness to pay and usage patterns:

- a six-to-eight-month outdoor season with an off-season pause or cancellation;
- a mixed outdoor/indoor year with reduced winter use;
- a year-round indoor or warm-weather-client practice; or
- a short seasonal peak where the first assessment opportunity may not occur during a fixed trial window.

`[REAL-WORLD VALIDATION REQUIRED]` Record active months, off-season behavior, data-access needs, preferred reactivation timing, and whether a pause is more useful than cancellation. Do not assume twelve paid months.

## Reasons this customer may buy

- One additional package or renewal may plausibly cover several months of the working fee.
- The instructor's expertise is stronger than the current presentation of it.
- The roadmap can differentiate service without replacing tools or methods.
- A standardized client experience may reduce ad hoc explanation and missed follow-up.
- Transparent self-serve purchase reduces procurement and setup friction.
- Coach branding and visible progress may support trust and referrals.

## Reasons this customer may refuse

- “My verbal close already works.”
- “I do not need another product.”
- “I do not have time to write a roadmap.”
- “A template will make my coaching feel generic.”
- “I will not use it enough in the off-season.”
- “My current platform already does this job.”
- “I do not want software shaping my recommendation.”
- “I cannot justify **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $75 per month**.”

## Conditions for willingness to pay

`[UNVALIDATED BUSINESS ASSUMPTION]` The target instructor is likely to pay only if the product:

1. is understandable and adoptable without a call;
2. reuses current assessment inputs;
3. remains visibly coach-owned and personal;
4. produces a credible roadmap within acceptable measured effort;
5. works with the existing purchase route;
6. creates a real package, renewal, or referral opportunity;
7. has simple cancellation and seasonality behavior; and
8. can plausibly return more value than its fee and support cost.

`[REAL-WORLD VALIDATION REQUIRED]` Use observed unassisted behavior, real roadmap shares, real payment choices, continued use, seasonal decisions, and measured support. Stated enthusiasm alone is insufficient.
