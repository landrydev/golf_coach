## Recommended business model

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]`

Use a Canada-wide, self-serve B2B SaaS subscription paid by an individual golf instructor. The product experience and unit economics must not depend on a sales call, custom proposal, mandatory setup fee, bespoke configuration, onboarding meeting, concierge service, or facility-by-facility enterprise sale.

The instructor retains the golfer relationship, coaching offer, transaction route, coaching revenue, methods, and tools. The product initially avoids revenue share, native payment processing, advertising, golfer subscriptions, and sale of golfer data.

Founder-led research, outreach, and assisted beta support are permitted as learning methods. They must be separated from normal acquisition and support in every economic report.

Offer one Solo Instructor plan at **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $75 per month per instructor**:

### Business North Star

> Trailing-twelve-month seasonalized recurring subscription revenue from activated instructors.

Before a complete year exists, report cohort MRR, activated paid instructors, and paid full-price months. Do not multiply peak-season MRR by twelve and present it as durable annual revenue.

### Customer-success metrics

- assessment-to-package conversion;
- 30-day coaching revenue per eligible assessed golfer;
- lessons purchased per golfer;
- package completion and renewal;
- instructor referral rate; and
- golfer comprehension, confidence, personal relevance, and trust.

## Metric dictionary

| Metric | Definition | Required segmentation |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified signup | New instructor account meeting the predeclared core ICP and having a plausible real assessment opportunity. | Channel, region, seasonal/year-round, trial cohort. |
| Complete roadmap | Minimum goal, starting point, priority barriers, phases, first phase, package connection, and valid next action pass the content checklist. | Template path, missing optional evidence, support used. |
| Activation | Qualified instructor shares at least one complete roadmap with the intended real golfer. | Human onboarding used or not, time, channel. |
| Human-free activation | Activated with no scheduled walkthrough, custom setup, founder-authored content, or task coaching. | Reactive help used and minutes. |
| Trial-to-paid | Eligible trial accounts beginning a paid subscription within the declared conversion window ÷ eligible trials. | Activated/not activated, real package opportunity, channel. |
| MRR | Full-price monthly subscription value plus pause subscription value, reported separately and combined. | Full, pause, new, expansion, contraction, reactivation. |
| Monthly logo churn | Paid accounts ending all paid states in month ÷ paid accounts at month start. | Seasonal/non-seasonal and voluntary/involuntary reason. |
| Paid full-price months/year | Count of months billed at the full solo price per customer in a rolling or seasonal year. | Instructor cohort and season type. |
| Seasonal reactivation | Eligible paused or seasonally cancelled instructors resuming next season ÷ eligible instructors expected to return. | Pause versus cancel, region, reminder exposure. |
| CAC | Attributable acquisition spending and labor ÷ new paid instructors from the channel. | Channel and cohort. |
| CAC payback | CAC ÷ monthly gross profit per new full-price instructor. | Channel and cohort. |
| Support time/account | Human minutes spent on normal product support ÷ active accounts; research and assisted-beta work reported separately. | Reason, pre/post activation, avoidable/unavoidable. |
| Assessment conversion | Eligible assessed golfers committing to a qualifying package within the declared window ÷ eligible assessed golfers. | Instructor, baseline/intervention, season, lead source. |

## Unit-economics formulas and interpretation

```text
Monthly recurring revenue
= full-price subscriptions × full price
+ pause subscriptions × pause price

Gross margin
= (subscription revenue − directly attributable service costs)
÷ subscription revenue

CAC payback months
= acquisition cost per paid instructor
÷ monthly gross profit per full-price instructor
```

`[REAL-WORLD VALIDATION REQUIRED]` Measure direct delivery cost, payment cost, support, media/storage or distribution cost, refunds, and other true variable costs without selecting technical architecture in this plan. Record founder research and assisted-beta labor separately, but never hide normal product work as “research.”

## Refusal and change triggers

Modify, pause, or reject the model if evidence shows that:

- instructors require recurring live explanation to understand the product;
- setup or roadmap creation remains bespoke founder work;
- standard templates cannot produce credible personal outputs;
- support or variable cost cannot fit the low price;
- qualified acquisition requires an uneconomical sales or advertising cost;
- real payment acceptance or retention at the working price is weak;
- seasonal cancellation has inadequate reactivation;
- product use does not reach real golfer shares or package opportunities;
- the model depends on facility contracts or team features to survive; or
- ethical, privacy, accessibility, or trust guardrails fail.

Do not rescue a failed self-serve test by quietly reclassifying founder labor as onboarding or by switching to custom facility work without an explicit new owner decision.
