## Boundary statement

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]`

The company is a coach-branded sales-enablement and client-experience layer above an instructor's existing coaching and operating tools. It helps one instructor independently curate coach-approved inputs into a clearer buying and development experience. It does not determine how the instructor coaches.

The first product must include enough acquisition, signup, setup, authoring, preview, share, and return experience to prove true self-serve activation. It must also include the golfer-facing assessment-to-package roadmap that delivers the commercial value.

## In scope for the first self-serve design specification

### Acquisition and account entry

- Landing-page problem, outcome, mechanism, compatibility, and effort explanation.
- Realistic sample roadmap and coach-input demonstration.
- Transparent **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $75 per month per instructor** presentation.
- FAQ covering existing tools, inputs, privacy expectations, trial, cancellation, and seasonality.
- Self-serve signup or candidate trial path.

### Instructor activation

- Instructor identity and basic brand inputs through standard controls.
- Current lesson-package definition and external booking, purchase, or contact link.
- Minimal golfer profile and desired outcome.
- Starting assessment, priority barriers, and three or four phases.
- Recommended first phase linked to the package.
- Golfer-view preview, edit/recovery, share, success, and minimal return state.
- Contextual help and optional examples that do not require human onboarding.

### Golfer roadmap

- Private, coach-branded welcome.
- Goal and personal reason.
- Honest starting point with strengths, patterns, selected evidence, and limits.
- Two or three priority barriers.
- Three or four directional phases.
- One recommended first phase with purpose and progress signals.
- One coach-owned package recommendation with visible terms.
- Link or handoff to the instructor's current purchase/contact route.
- Visible question, wait, decline, unavailable-action, limited-data, error, privacy, accessibility, and responsive states.

## Explicit product non-goals

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]`

- Booking, scheduling, native payment processing, invoicing, or general CRM.
- Swing diagnosis, coaching recommendation generation, or performance prediction.
- A coaching curriculum, certification system, or autonomous coach.
- Launch-monitor, video-capture, annotation, analysis, or communication replacement.
- Public coach marketplace, social feed, GPS product, simulator, or facility operations.
- Complete academy management or multi-coach administration in the initial product.
- Custom website building, bespoke branding projects, or managed report production.
- Enterprise procurement, custom proposals, mandatory setup fees, or concierge onboarding.
- Guaranteed golfer outcomes, package sales, renewals, referrals, or timing.
- Required public sharing or sale of golfer data.

## Experience boundaries

- The coach owns every diagnosis, priority, phase, interpretation, and recommendation.
- Standard templates guide structure but do not write unreviewed coaching judgments.
- Optional content never blocks the shortest credible roadmap.
- Evidence is curated; raw-data completeness is not the goal.
- One current priority and one recommended package lead.
- Alternatives and a non-purchase path remain visible.
- Progress language can show uncertainty, no change, or setback.
- Private is the default; sharing, correction, access, and revocation expectations require explicit design.
- A complete coach dashboard is deferred unless a component is necessary to return to and manage the first roadmap.

## Commercial boundaries

- The individual instructor pays the subscription; the golfer pays the instructor through an existing route.
- The recommended initial offer has no mandatory setup fee, custom quote, or long contract.
- The initial model does not depend on revenue share, ads, golfer subscriptions, or selling data.
- Direct golf-course or facility contracts are not the initial commercial motion.
- Founder outreach may recruit research or lead to the self-serve path; a founder's continued involvement is not a product requirement.
- Package framing may improve sales through clarity, but no dark patterns, false urgency, artificial scarcity, fear, shame, or hidden alternative are permitted.
- A non-purchase outcome is valid when the plan, timing, evidence, or golfer preference does not support continuing.

## Information boundaries

- Collect only the information required for the approved experience.
- Do not place identifiable participant or golfer records in this documentation repository.
- Do not use junior-golfer data before safeguarding, consent, and privacy requirements are resolved.
- Do not infer sensitive characteristics or coaching conclusions.
- Retention, deletion, correction, access, media consent, share revocation, subscription cancellation, and pause behavior require policy and qualified review before real use.
- No technical storage or architecture decision is made in this pre-code plan.

## Boundary exception test

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`

A proposed capability may cross a boundary only when all questions have evidence-backed answers:

1. Which named activation, conversion, renewal, referral, retention, or economic outcome is blocked?
2. What observed instructor or golfer behavior proves the need?
3. Why can an existing tool, link, standard template, or narrower manual research method not perform the job?
4. What coach effort, support burden, privacy risk, cost, or complexity does it add?
5. What narrower alternative was tested and failed?
6. Does it preserve self-service at **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $75 per month**?
7. Which decision, scope, wireframe, validation, handoff, and gate artifacts must change?

Convenience, competitor parity, a facility request, or technical preference is insufficient.

## Boundary scenarios

| Request | Current disposition | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Paste a link to the coach's checkout or booking page | In scope | Uses the existing route and tests the package decision. |
| Build native checkout | Out of scope | Transaction ownership is unnecessary for the first wedge. |
| Configure a logo, coach name, and brand accent | In scope | Standard basic branding supports coach ownership. |
| Offer a custom brand project | Out of scope | Creates bespoke service dependence. |
| Show a coach-selected swing clip with explanation | In scope when consent and policy are resolved | It is curated evidence. |
| Add swing annotation tools | Out of scope | Replaces an existing analysis function. |
| Generate a diagnosis or roadmap phases autonomously | Out of scope | The coach must own coaching judgment. |
| Add multi-coach roles for an early facility prospect | Later expansion | Team administration must not distort solo activation. |
| Keep a paused account read-only | Candidate seasonality scope | Supports retention and reactivation; exact policy is unvalidated. |
