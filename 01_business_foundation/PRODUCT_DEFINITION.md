> We help independent golf instructors sell more lesson packages, renew more golfers, and earn more referrals by turning the goals, observations, videos, drills, and measurements they already collect into a personalized, coach-branded development roadmap—through a plug-and-play product they can start using without a sales call or onboarding meeting.

## Exact product definition

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]`

The product is a standardized, coach-branded presentation layer above the instructor's existing workflow. The instructor supplies or approves the golfer's goal, starting observations, selected evidence, priority barriers, development sequence, first-phase purpose, progress signals, package recommendation, and next action. The product helps the instructor curate those inputs into a coherent buying and development story.

The company does not diagnose the swing, select methodology, prescribe a coaching plan, promise outcomes, or replace the coach's judgment. The instructor remains the expert, author, and accountable source of the recommendation.

## Two connected first-product jobs

### Job A — instructor self-serve activation

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`

```text
Understand the promise and price
→ inspect a realistic sample roadmap
→ sign up or begin a trial
→ add coach identity and basic branding
→ define a current lesson package and next-action link
→ add the first golfer and assessment
→ shape the development phases
→ preview the golfer experience
→ share it
→ return to the product
```

The normal path cannot require a call, custom proposal, mandatory onboarding meeting, founder-built template, or manual implementation.

### Job B — golfer package decision

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]`

```text
Private welcome
→ desired outcome
→ starting point
→ most important barriers
→ development roadmap
→ recommended first phase
→ lesson-package recommendation
→ clear next action
```

The golfer must be able to understand the recommendation, ask a question, wait, or decline without hidden terms or pressure.

## Primary users and jobs

| Actor | Job to be done | Product response |
|---|---|---|
| Economic buyer and primary business user: independent instructor | Turn legitimate assessment value into a package opportunity without adding a heavy administrative or sales process. | Transparent self-serve offer, guided setup, standardized authoring, preview, share, and a minimal return state. |
| End user: adult golfer | Decide whether the coach understands the goal, has a credible sequence, and has explained the recommended package. | Personal context, coach reasoning, selected evidence, honest limits, one priority, and an unpressured next action. |
| Returning golfer | See what matters, what changed, and whether another phase is worthwhile. | Preserve the roadmap and later add selected progress, completion, renewal, and referral moments after validation. |

No facility administrator, academy buyer, implementation specialist, or founder is required in the initial operating model.

## Product lifecycle

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]`

1. **Activate the instructor:** explain, configure, author, preview, and share without human onboarding.
2. **Convert:** assessment to roadmap to initial phase/package decision.
3. **Reassure:** connect early coaching activity to the promised plan.
4. **Demonstrate:** show current priority, practice direction, and representative evidence.
5. **Renew:** review the completed phase before recommending the next one.
6. **Refer:** offer a private-by-default, earned progress story the golfer may choose to share.

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]` The first design and prototype program must cover instructor activation and the golfer conversion flow. Later lifecycle moments remain specified but do not enter the first product merely because they are documented.

## Activation and success definition

### Product activation

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`

> An instructor creates and shares a complete golfer roadmap connected to a current lesson package, without human onboarding.

Draft creation, brand setup, and preview are useful leading signals. Sharing a complete, real-use roadmap is the first evidence-bearing value event.

### Golfer success

A representative golfer should be able to say, in their own words:

> “My coach understood the golf I want to play, explained what is getting in the way, showed why this work comes first, and made the recommended package easy to evaluate without pressuring me.”

### Business success

Success requires evidence of both:

- self-serve SaaS health: signup, activation, trial-to-paid conversion, recurring revenue, retention, seasonal behavior, acquisition cost, payback, and low support dependence; and
- instructor outcomes: assessment-to-package conversion, revenue per assessed golfer, renewal, lessons purchased, and referrals.

Customer outcome measures do not replace SaaS operating measures.

## Product promise and non-promises

### Promise

Turn a real assessment into a personal, credible development story that helps the golfer understand and act on an appropriate first phase—without asking the instructor to replace existing tools or buy a managed service.

### Explicit non-promises

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]`

- Guaranteed handicap, score, swing, conversion, renewal, referral, or revenue outcomes.
- A universal coaching method or automated coaching diagnosis.
- A prediction of how many sessions a golfer will need.
- Replacement of booking, payment, messaging, video analysis, launch monitors, or coach judgment.
- A general CRM, academy-management product, custom website builder, or marketplace.
- Bespoke implementation, ongoing concierge service, or facility-level enterprise delivery.
