## Screen 1 — Phase-completion review

### Purpose and desired response

- **Screen purpose:** show what the purchased phase intended to do, what occurred, and how reliable the change is.
- **Golfer question:** “What did my CAD $595 phase actually accomplish?”
- **Commercial purpose:** make delivered value visible and earn permission to discuss what follows.
- **Desired emotion:** recognized, accurately informed, respected.

### Information hierarchy and example content

1. Status: **“Start-Line Control · Phase complete at the defined practice objective.”**
2. Original purpose: **make the driver's initial direction more predictable at manageable speed before adding speed.**
3. Starting condition: best drives playable; severe right start present under speed; assessment set **4 / 3 / 3**.
4. Work completed:
   - three coaching sessions over six weeks;
   - two focused practice prescriptions completed by Mark;
   - repeated start-window observation at controlled and increasing speed;
   - representative clips and small-sample outcome counts.
5. What changed:
   - Mark can explain and use the start-window task;
   - two controlled sets recorded **7 / 2 / 1**;
   - severe right starts appeared less often in that context.
6. Reliability label: **“Repeated in controlled practice.”**
7. What is not yet proven:
   - reliability at full playing speed;
   - transfer across targets and pressure;
   - sustained reduction in on-course penalty shots or scoring.
8. Golfer contribution: **“Mark completed two focused sessions and reported the on-course results honestly, including the difficult round.”**
9. Maya's conclusion:

   > “The start-line objective is repeatable enough in controlled practice to close this phase. It is not yet a course-ready driver pattern. The next decision is whether to build that pattern with coached variation or practise this task independently before reassessment.”

### Actions and presence

- **Primary action:** “Review Maya's next-phase recommendation.”
- **Secondary actions:** inspect evidence, review completed lesson chapters, “I want to discuss this conclusion.”
- **Coach presence:** Maya owns phase status, reliability, limitations, and conclusion; a confirmation date is visible.
- **Evidence required:** original baseline, completed work, representative change, reliability, limit, and coach conclusion. Without these, no “complete” label appears.
- **Deliberately omitted:** next-package price above the fold, renewal banner, confetti, inflated percentage improvement, fear of regression.

### Responsive, states, access, and ethics

- **Mobile:** linear story—purpose → baseline → work → change → reliability → limits → conclusion. Evidence expands only after the claim/limit is understood.
- **Desktop:** baseline/current evidence may sit side by side; conclusion follows, not beside a sales panel.
- **Limited evidence:** use “phase review” with status **incomplete**, **paused**, or **revised**; explain why. Do not call it complete to enable renewal.
- **Partial completion:** separate accomplished elements from remaining original work; the next recommendation may extend or revise rather than invent a new phase.
- **Setback at completion:** show current setback alongside prior progress and let Maya choose pause/reassess.
- **Error/no media:** text review and evidence labels remain complete; no empty gallery.
- **Accessibility:** text comparison, semantic sections, no colour-only reliability, media alternatives, reduced-motion equivalent, 200% one-column reflow.
- **Ethical risk:** selecting only flattering evidence or using sunk-cost framing. Limitations and non-purchase suitability must remain prominent.
- **Completion check:** golfer can explain original objective, baseline, work, change, reliability, limitation, and Maya's conclusion before seeing price.

## Screen 2 — Remaining opportunity and next-phase recommendation

### Purpose and desired response

- **Screen purpose:** connect the completed evidence to a distinct next coaching chapter.
- **Golfer question:** “What comes next, why is it different, and do I need coaching now?”
- **Commercial purpose:** make renewal a logical, evidence-backed choice.
- **Desired emotion:** clear, confident, free to choose.

### Information hierarchy and example content

1. Remaining opportunity: **“Turn a controlled start line into a playable driver pattern at normal speed and varied targets.”**
2. Evidence basis: repeated controlled practice plus recurring full-speed/course variability.
3. Recommended next phase: **“Playable Driver Pattern.”**
4. Purpose: **reduce the severe right miss while building one stock pattern Mark can recognize and use.**
5. Why it follows: **start direction is now repeatable enough in controlled practice to introduce speed, target variability, and decision context without skipping the foundation.**
6. Progress signals:
   - maintain the start window across varied speeds and targets;
   - reduce severe right misses across repeated mixed practice sets;
   - begin testing the pattern on course without claiming scoring transfer.
7. Expectations: one focused weekly practice plus two on-course observation notes where feasible.
8. Alternative: **independent practice with a defined reassessment point after two practice sets or four weeks**, if Maya considers that safe and appropriate.

### Actions and presence

- **Primary action:** “See the Playable Driver Pattern option.”
- **Secondary actions:** “Choose independent practice first,” “Ask Maya a question,” Back to review.
- **Coach presence:** rationale and suitability are stated in Maya's voice.
- **Evidence required:** explicit link from completed-phase evidence and remaining limitation to the next purpose.
- **Deliberately omitted:** generic “keep improving,” fear of losing progress, package price before rationale, guaranteed course/scoring result.

### Responsive, states, access, and ethics

- **Mobile/desktop:** recommendation and independent path share the same section; the independent path is not hidden in fine print.
- **No distinct next phase:** show “Continue independent practice and reassess” as the recommendation; omit a package.
- **Coach uncertain:** label it a reassessment recommendation and show what evidence is needed.
- **Accessibility:** all choices keyboard reachable, distinct descriptive labels, no time limit, selected path announced in text.
- **Ethical risk:** treating every completed phase as a renewal opportunity. The coach must be able to recommend no purchase.
- **Completion check:** golfer explains the new purpose, evidence basis, how it differs, and the independent alternative.

## Screen 3 — Next-phase package and decision

### Purpose and desired response

- **Screen purpose:** present complete next-phase terms only after the review and rationale.
- **Golfer question:** “What would continuing involve, what does it cost, and what happens if I wait?”
- **Commercial purpose:** convert an earned next-phase recommendation using the instructor's existing route.
- **Desired emotion:** informed and in control.

### Information hierarchy and example content

1. Package: **“Playable Driver Pattern Coaching Phase.”**
2. Synthetic price: **CAD $745**.
3. Includes:
   - four coaching sessions over an estimated seven to nine weeks;
   - one focused practice direction after each session;
   - mixed-speed and target evidence selected by Maya;
   - one course-transfer check where feasible; and
   - end-of-phase review.
4. Fit explanation: four sessions provide repeated coached variation and a review; they do not guarantee a score or eliminate every right miss.
5. Important synthetic terms: exact session duration, expiry, cancellation/rescheduling, and taxes must be replaced by the instructor's current terms before a real test.
6. Completion choices: continue to On-Course Transfer, revise, pause, or practise independently—no automatic renewal.
7. Existing-system handoff notice.

### Actions and presence

- **Primary action:** “Begin Playable Driver Pattern” → existing instructor route; prototype shows a handoff preview only.
- **Secondary:** “Ask Maya,” “Practise independently first,” “Review later,” Back.
- **Coach presence:** recommendation and package fit belong to Maya; price/terms belong to her business.
- **Evidence required:** completed review and next-phase rationale; no popularity or scarcity proof.
- **Deliberately omitted:** crossed-out price, countdown, “do not lose progress,” automatic renewal, confusing option grid.

### Responsive, states, access, and ethics

- **Mobile:** rationale is already understood; package still shows price, fit, inclusions, terms, completion, then actions.
- **Desktop:** package may sit beside a concise evidence-basis recap, never dominate the prior review.
- **Action unavailable:** keep the explanation and show direct contact; never imply a reserved place.
- **Wait/independent:** neutral confirmation and reassessment guidance; no guilt modal or repeated prompt.
- **Error:** state clearly that no booking/payment occurred.
- **Accessibility:** currency and terms are text, focus order reaches alternatives, external handoff warning, status announcement, 200% reflow.
- **Ethical risk:** using continuity to make renewal feel compulsory. Explicit no-auto-renewal and independent-practice path are mandatory.
- **Completion check:** golfer can state price, inclusions, distinct purpose, terms, completion, and every reasonable choice.

## Phase-status variants

| Status | Required language and action |
|---|---|
| Complete | Show evidence/reliability and a distinct next decision. |
| Partially complete | Separate accomplished and unfinished original objectives; recommend extend/revise/pause only with rationale. |
| Paused | State reason and what guidance remains current; no urgency. |
| Revised | Explain what new evidence changed the plan; preserve original phase record. |
| Insufficient evidence | Do not claim completion; schedule observation/reassessment or recommend independent practice. |
| Goal changed | Re-anchor to the confirmed goal before recommending a phase. |
