## Flow objective

Answer:

> “Is this achievement meaningful, accurately represented, private unless I choose otherwise, and worth sharing?”

The golfer's achievement leads. Sharing and referral are separate, optional actions after a valuable private milestone exists.

## Role in the self-serve business

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]` Earned golfer milestones and instructor referrals remain later growth mechanisms. Milestone preparation must be standardized and low effort; a human concierge cannot curate normal account output. Product referral must be separated from golfer-content sharing so Canada-wide acquisition never depends on exposing private client evidence.

`[UNVALIDATED BUSINESS ASSUMPTION]` A privacy-respecting milestone will create meaningful golfer or instructor referral contribution. Real behavior and qualified policy review are required.

## Screen 1 — Private milestone

### Purpose and desired response

- **Screen purpose:** make earned progress memorable before mentioning sharing.
- **Golfer question:** “What happened, why does it matter, and is the claim honest?”
- **Commercial purpose:** reinforce value and create authentic advocacy readiness.
- **Desired emotion:** earned pride, recognition, perspective.

### Information hierarchy and example content

1. Private label: **“A milestone in your development journey.”**
2. Achievement: **“89 — your first sub-90 round in this journey.”**
3. Goal connection: **“You wanted driver penalties to stop deciding rounds with friends.”**
4. Evidence: recorded scorecard supports 89; Mark reported zero driver penalty shots; Maya reviewed the round context.
5. Maya's recognition:

   > “This round matters because the driver stayed playable enough for your scoring strengths to count. It is one round, not proof that the pattern is permanent, but it is the on-course outcome we have been building toward.”

6. Before-and-now context: typical score **94–101** and self-reported **3–5 driver penalties** versus this round's **89** and **0 reported driver penalties**.
7. Limitation: **single-round milestone; continued course evidence is needed.**
8. Next opportunity: **repeat the decision and driver pattern across different rounds without chasing the score.**

### Actions and presence

- **Primary action:** “Save to my private journey.”
- **Secondary action:** “Choose whether to share”; “Not now.” Sharing never leads by default.
- **Coach presence:** Maya confirms significance and provides the recognition; branding is restrained.
- **Evidence required:** named outcome, source, context, coach confirmation, and limit.
- **Deliberately omitted:** confetti by default, points/badges, public count, referral request, exaggerated “transformation,” guaranteed future scores.

### Responsive, states, access, and ethics

- **Mobile:** milestone, meaning, coach note, evidence/limit, then choices; a composed static reveal works without motion.
- **Desktop:** evidence comparison may sit beside the narrative; achievement remains golfer-first.
- **Limited evidence:** use the verified part only. If the score is recorded but penalty attribution is uncertain, omit or label it clearly.
- **Milestone rejected by coach:** no golfer-facing celebration; return to ordinary evidence or progress content.
- **No media:** milestone remains complete; a swing clip is optional and not the proof of the score.
- **Accessibility:** heading conveys the milestone; no motion-only reveal; score and evidence labels are text; reduced motion is the default-equivalent experience.
- **Ethical risk:** converting a single best round into a normal-performance claim. The limitation must remain adjacent.
- **Completion check:** golfer can explain achievement, significance, evidence source, limitation, and that it remains private.

## Screen 2 — Share selection and complete preview

### Purpose and desired response

- **Screen purpose:** let Mark deliberately choose exactly what may leave the private journey.
- **Golfer question:** “What would be shared, with whom, and what stays private?”
- **Commercial purpose:** enable earned visibility without compromising control or trust.
- **Desired emotion:** proud, informed, in control.

### Information hierarchy and example content

1. Privacy status: **“Nothing is shared yet.”**
2. Content selection, all off by default in the specification until legal/privacy review determines the valid consent pattern:
   - First name: **Mark**.
   - Milestone: **First sub-90 round — 89**.
   - Context: **No driver penalty shots reported in this round**.
   - Coach recognition excerpt.
   - Before/current swing clip: **not selected by default**.
   - Exact scorecard image: **not selected by default**.
   - Coach brand: **Bennett Golf Coaching** in a secondary position.
3. Required limitation included with any shared story: **“One-round milestone; future consistency not implied.”**
4. Full artifact preview showing every visible word, image, logo, and link.
5. Audience/link explanation, expiry, download, onward sharing, revoke/delete, and metadata rules: **awaiting qualified legal/privacy decision; no real sharing may occur until resolved.**

### Actions and presence

- **Primary action:** “Create a private preview.” This does not publish.
- **Secondary actions:** “Keep private,” Back to milestone, clear selection.
- **Coach presence:** approved recognition and restrained brand; coach cannot override Mark's sharing choice.
- **Evidence required:** only coach-approved source content may be selectable.
- **Deliberately omitted:** preselected social network, public default, contacts access, automatic post, referral prompt, hidden tracking, another golfer's content.

### Responsive, states, access, and ethics

- **Mobile:** selection and preview are sequential; a summary states the number/type of selected items; keep-private action is always visible.
- **Desktop:** selection and preview may sit side by side with the same reading/focus order.
- **Sharing unavailable:** milestone remains private and valuable; say why without exposing system details.
- **Media unavailable:** remove the item and update preview; never substitute another clip automatically.
- **Consent/revocation unknown:** block real sharing; do not simulate legal completeness.
- **Error:** clearly state that nothing was published; preserve private original and allow return.
- **Accessibility:** every selection has a full text label, state, and description; preview reading order is logical; no drag-only controls; 200% reflow; image text repeated as real text.
- **Ethical risk:** using defaults or confusing previews to expand disclosure. Off-by-default, granular choice, complete preview, and clear non-public status are mandatory.
- **Completion check:** golfer can list selected items, omitted private items, audience/link behaviour once defined, and how to keep everything private.

## Screen 3 — Optional share and separate referral

### Purpose and desired response

- **Screen purpose:** complete a deliberate share or referral only after preview and consent; preserve separate choices.
- **Golfer question:** “Am I sharing my achievement, referring someone, both, or neither?”
- **Commercial purpose:** generate authentic qualified awareness and referrals without converting the milestone into an advertisement.
- **Desired emotion:** voluntary advocacy with no obligation.

### Information hierarchy and example content

1. Final share summary: exact selected content and destination/link behaviour.
2. Share action: **“Share this milestone”** only after real privacy/legal requirements and consent design are approved.
3. Keep-private action: equal access and no persuasive confirmation modal.
4. Referral divider: **“Know someone who would value a clear coaching plan?”**
5. Referral explanation: what information, if any, would be sent; the referred person's data is not entered or contacted without their consent under the future approved process.
6. Separate actions:
   - **“Copy a general assessment invitation”** with no private milestone details unless selected.
   - **“Skip referral.”**
7. Confirmation states explicitly distinguish preview created, share completed/failed, and referral invitation copied/not sent.

### Actions and presence

- **Primary action:** depends on Mark's selected intent; there is no universal commercial primary action.
- **Secondary:** keep private, skip referral, revoke where legally/technically supported in a future approved design.
- **Coach presence:** brand remains secondary; no coach-authored pressure or incentive.
- **Evidence required:** approved milestone content and valid consent state.
- **Deliberately omitted:** automatic contact upload, referral bounty by default, public leaderboard, social proof count, bundled share-and-refer consent.

### Responsive, states, access, and ethics

- **Mobile/desktop:** share and referral are visually and semantically separate; neither is preselected.
- **Share failure:** announce “Not shared” and provide retry/keep-private; never show a misleading success preview.
- **Referral skipped:** close neutrally and return to private milestone.
- **Revocation:** future behavior must explain what can be withdrawn and what onward recipients may already retain; legal review required.
- **Accessibility:** clear state announcements, no clipboard-only feedback, keyboard access, plain-language consent, no time limit.
- **Ethical risk:** conflating pride with obligation or using the golfer's story as coach-first advertising. The milestone remains useful when both actions are declined.
- **Completion check:** golfer understands the difference between share and referral, knows what happened, and can choose neither without friction.

## Milestone eligibility checklist

- [ ] Specific achievement rather than vague “improvement.”
- [ ] Direct connection to the golfer's goal or active phase.
- [ ] Evidence source and context named.
- [ ] Coach confirms significance and wording.
- [ ] Limitation or maturity level included.
- [ ] Infrequent enough to remain meaningful.
- [ ] Private version valuable without sharing.
- [ ] No sensitive or third-party information included.

## Never share without a separate approved rule

[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]

- Full legal name, email, phone, exact location, schedule, account details, or payment information.
- Unselected swing clips, lesson notes, coach annotations, health/injury information, or other golfers.
- Full scorecard/location metadata unless explicitly selected and legally appropriate.
- Junior information in the initial scope.
- A claim stronger than the private evidence supports.
