## Classification labels

- `[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]` — directly grounded in the current authoritative [BUSINESS_PLAN_V2.md](BUSINESS_PLAN_V2.md).
- `[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]` — a decisive candidate recommendation for planning; not Aaron-approved.
- `[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION]` — a price, discount, pause fee, or packaging amount that has not earned willingness-to-pay evidence.
- `[UNVALIDATED BUSINESS ASSUMPTION]` — a plausible belief that needs real customer, workflow, commercial, operational, seasonal, or usability evidence.
- `[REAL-WORLD VALIDATION REQUIRED]` — exact evidence or qualified professional review still needed.
- `[EXTERNAL EVIDENCE — source, access date]` — a sourced external fact, distinct from inference or recommendation.
