## Decision standard

Acceptance means the exact version supports unaided product comprehension, self-serve task completion, credible coach-owned output, golfer comprehension, and ethical choices in tested modes. It does not validate demand, payment, commercial lift, retention, seasonality, support economics, compliance, production feasibility, or coding readiness.

All thresholds are `[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`. Use 8 previously unexposed qualified instructors, 10 golfers, and 5 coach-authenticity reviewers. Zero unresolved critical findings are permitted.

## Finding limits

- **Critical:** zero unresolved. Includes product/price misunderstanding, task-blocking self-serve failure, product-authored diagnosis, hidden charge/cancel, unsafe share, golfer misunderstanding, pressure, privacy/access failure.
- **Major:** no more than two unresolved overall, neither affecting activation, price/terms, coach ownership, package understanding, alternatives, privacy, or access. Aaron explicitly accepts/schedules before later beta planning.
- **Moderate/minor:** recorded with owner/retest; repeated moderate issues may be promoted.

## Decision outcomes

- **ACCEPT FOR DESIGN-GATE CONSIDERATION:** thresholds met, findings within limits, exact version approved by Aaron; code remains unauthorized until gate signature.
- **REVISE AND RETEST:** bounded problems are fixable within model/scope.
- **RETURN TO STRATEGY:** proposition, self-service, standardization, package trust, or scope fails structurally.
- **STOP:** manipulation, unsafe privacy/access, hidden terms, or recurring founder dependence cannot be corrected within boundaries.
