## Recommended development roadmap

1. **Start-Line Control** — make initial direction more predictable at manageable speed.
2. **Playable Driver Pattern** — reduce the severe right miss and build one recognizable stock pattern.
3. **On-Course Transfer** — test the pattern across targets, pressure, and club-choice decisions.
4. **Scoring Consolidation** — compare driver penalties with approach and short-game evidence before choosing the next scoring priority.

Future phases are directional. Maya may revise them as evidence changes.

## Recommended first phase

### Start-Line Control

- **Objective:** make the driver's initial direction more predictable before adding speed.
- **Why first:** it creates a stable pattern Maya and Mark can evaluate before introducing speed and course variability.
- **What success could look like:** Mark can explain the task; more shots begin within Maya's defined start window across repeated controlled sets; severe right starts occur less often in that context.
- **Golfer commitment:** one 30–45 minute focused session most weeks and a short pre-round rehearsal.
- **Outside the phase:** no promise to break 90, eliminate every right miss, rebuild every full-swing detail, or prove course transfer.
- **Completion:** coach-confirmed review after the third session; options are close the phase, revise/extend, pause, or practise independently.
