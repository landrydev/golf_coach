### FAQ

**What will this help me sell?**

> It helps a golfer understand why a structured first coaching phase and its connected lesson package make sense after an assessment. It does not guarantee a sale.

**Do I have to change my coaching method?**

> No. You provide and approve the goal, assessment, barriers, phases, evidence, progress signals, and package fit. The product organizes your judgment; it does not diagnose or coach the golfer.

**Do I need a launch monitor or swing video?**

> No. Use video, measurements, or launch data only when they help explain the decision. A concise coach-observation version must remain credible.

**What does the golfer receive?**

> A private, coach-branded roadmap showing their goal, starting point, priority barriers, development phases, recommended first phase, connected package, and clear next choices.

**Does Roadmap handle booking or payment?**

> No. Add the existing booking, purchase, or contact link you already use. The roadmap explains the recommendation and then hands the golfer to that process.

**How quickly can I create the first roadmap?**

> The working usability target is 30 active minutes or less for a qualified instructor. That target is untested and is not a product promise.

**Can I cancel when my season ends?**

> The initial concept is month to month with easy cancellation and an optional read-only seasonal pause. Exact policy and data access still require review.

**Is this for academies or multi-coach facilities?**

> The first concept is for one independent instructor. A later self-serve team tier may be considered after the solo experience is proven.

**Can I talk to someone?**

> Product help may be available, but a sales call or onboarding meeting is not required to understand or begin the product.

## ACT-04 — Starting assessment

**Step label**

> Setup 4 of 5 · Starting assessment

**Heading**

> Capture the few observations that explain what comes first.

**Strength field**

> Mark's best drives provide enough usable distance for his scoring goal, and his controlled-tempo mid-iron contact is a strength to preserve.

**Primary pattern field**

> At comfortable speed, several drives start in a usable window. As speed rises, the ball can start right and curve farther right. Aiming left and slowing down protects some right misses but introduces pulls.

**Priority barriers**

1. `Unpredictable start direction`
2. `Protective aim-and-speed compensation`
3. `Course transfer not yet proven`

**Optional evidence**

- `Coach observation during the assessment`
- `Two fictional representative clips`
- `Ten-ball note: 4 playable · 3 recoverable · 3 likely penalty`
- `Golfer report: 3–5 driver penalty shots in a typical round`

**Evidence-limit note**

> Ten shots are a small sample. No launch-monitor data was collected. On-course penalty frequency is self-reported. This evidence does not predict when Mark will break 90.
