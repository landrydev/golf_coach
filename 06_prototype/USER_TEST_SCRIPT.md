## Test purpose

Evaluate whether a previously unexposed instructor can understand and complete the self-serve first-roadmap flow, whether output represents coach judgment, and whether a golfer can understand/act without pressure.

This test does not prove live signup, willingness to pay, commercial lift, retention, support economics, production feasibility, or accessibility conformance.

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`

- 8 previously unexposed qualified instructors for acquisition/activation.
- 10 adult target-context golfers.
- 5 qualified coach-authenticity reviewers.
- Assign narrow/wide and access/state conditions.

Record participant code, qualification/seasonality, artifact/version/condition, first click, path, active/elapsed time, errors, backtracks, abandonment, recovery, every intervention/accommodation, exact unaided retell, content quality, price comprehension, and severity/evidence link.

## Moderator setup

- Synthetic content only; no real account, charge, send, booking, pause, or cancellation.
- Do not teach the proposition/navigation.
- Provide tasks, not walkthroughs.
- Record think-aloud method and all assistance.
- Accessibility accommodations are accommodations, not product assistance.
- Stop for distress, real-data exposure, unsafe-share interpretation, or consent withdrawal.

## Instructor session

### Opening

> Imagine you found this from a community, search, partner, referral, or direct message. Decide whether it fits your instruction business. I will not explain the product, and nothing creates an account or sends anything.

Do not mention roadmap, price, trial, or activation before discovery.

### A1 — Understand

> Decide what this product is, whether it might be for you, and what you would inspect next.

Before correction ask: who is it for, what problem, instructor action, golfer result, business outcome, and tools replaced?

### A2 — Sample

> Decide whether this example could be produced for one of your golfers and what you would need.

Ask which content comes from coach/product, what is required/optional, and what seems generic or custom-made.

### A3 — Price and terms

> Find cost, inclusion, how to begin, and what you could do off-season.

Retell **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $75/month**, setup/contract, candidate trial paid choice, cancellation, and candidate pause/resume. Record comprehension before opinion.

### A4 — Identity

> Start making one roadmap using the synthetic identity.

Assign no-logo or invalid-brand variant. Observe optional-content interpretation.

### A5 — Package

> Add the fictional package and make clear what it supports and where the golfer goes next.

Assign invalid link/incomplete package; observe recovery and native-payment expectations.

### A6 — Golfer and assessment

> Use provided synthetic facts. Create the minimum honest starting point without adding facts.

Observe example copying, writing effort, evidence choice, strengths/barriers, help.

### A7 — Phases

> Create the sequence, choose what comes first, and connect it to the package.

Ask why first, what future phases mean, and who made the recommendation.

### A8 — Preview and share

> Review what the golfer receives, correct the assigned problem, and share in the simulation when ready.

Observe blocker/omission comprehension, recipient/context, no-real-send notice, and accurate success.

### A9 — Return and seasonality

> Return, find the next useful action, then inspect what you would do when your season ends.

Retell recent work, next action, account state, pause/cancel consequence, resume expectation.

### Instructor debrief

- What did this help sell?
- Where would a person be required?
- Which input was unnecessary?
- Did output feel coach-owned?
- What would make it generic?
- What event makes continued payment worthwhile?
- What causes cancellation after first use?
- How does seasonality change the choice?
- What do you compare the working price with?

Do not turn debrief into onboarding or sales.

## Golfer session

### Opening

> Imagine your coach sent this after an assessment. Nothing you choose will book or charge anything.

### G1 — Orient

Open and identify whose plan, purpose, and first action.

### G2 — Goal/starting point

Retell goal, strength, observation, and uncertainty.

### G3 — Plan

Retell first phase, rationale, future-phase meaning, and progress signals.

### G4 — Package

Explain recommendation, inclusions, fictional coach price/terms, and phase connection. Check it is not confused with SaaS price.

### G5 — Next step

Choose any reasonable continue/ask/wait/decline action. Do not reward purchase.

### G6 — Recover

Assign unavailable link or incorrect goal and observe recovery.

### G7 — Limited/access condition

Assign text-only, no-media, narrow/zoom, keyboard, reduced-motion, or unauthorized variant.

### Golfer debrief

Retell goal, starting point, first phase, package purpose/terms/alternatives; identify substantive personal details, coach ownership, evidence limit, pressure/trust, questions, and expected privacy/access.

## Coach-authenticity review

After recent-workflow baseline:

1. map a recent assessment to required inputs;
2. identify method-prescriptive/generic content;
3. approve/reject each coaching judgment/package fit;
4. perform or estimate bounded authoring with limitations recorded; and
5. identify support, custom work, optional/deferred fields.

Ask whether the recommendation survives without a conversion target.

## Accessibility review tasks

- Keyboard acquisition-to-share and golfer path.
- Visible focus/logical order.
- Narrow/zoom/reflow with long content.
- No-media/reduced-motion equivalence.
- Form label/instruction/error association.
- Share failure/unauthorized privacy state.
- Trial/paid/pause/cancel/resume comprehension.

Record what Figma cannot validate.

## Moderator rules

- No business explanation before retell.
- No pointing to CTA, sample, price, optional label, error, or alternative.
- Do not finish instructor content.
- Assisted tasks do not count human-free.
- Price opinion is not willingness-to-pay evidence.
- Prototype response is not conversion.
- Probe behavior before preference; preserve contradictions/abandonment.

## Severity and close

- **Critical:** product/price/coach/package/privacy/choice/share/cancel/access misunderstanding or task block.
- **Major:** significant friction requiring help.
- **Moderate:** recoverable issue with meaningful effort/confidence cost.
- **Minor:** polish/low-impact clarity.

Close by explaining planning status and confirming no real operation occurred. Promise no launch, price, access, or capability.
