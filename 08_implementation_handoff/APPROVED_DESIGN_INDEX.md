1. exact artifact and version;
2. durable location;
3. Aaron decision ID/date;
4. evidence and unresolved risks reviewed;
5. approved scope and conditions;
6. superseded artifact, if any; and
7. revisit trigger.

Exploratory Figma work, simulated recommendations, text completeness, participant preference, or a “looks good” message is insufficient.
