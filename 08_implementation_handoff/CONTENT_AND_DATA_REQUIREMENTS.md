## Instructor activation content

### Coach identity

- Required: display name and minimum professional/contact identity.
- Optional: logo, photo, short philosophy, constrained accent.
- Deferred: custom fonts/layouts, facility hierarchy, multiple brands/coaches.

### Package

- Required before share: package name, purpose/fit, current price/currency or truthful current-details state, material inclusions, external action/contact route.
- Optional: cadence, practice expectation, evaluation, concise terms.
- Deferred: native checkout, discounting, inventory, package comparison, team billing.

### Golfer and goal

- Required: approved display identifier and primary outcome.
- Optional: why it matters, context, constraints, typical score/handicap if relevant.
- Deferred: broad demographics, contact/CRM history, irrelevant personal data.

### Assessment and evidence

- Required: starting statement, at least one strength, primary pattern, one priority barrier.
- Optional: up to three selected evidence items with source/context/limit.
- Deferred: raw data inventory, full media archive, automated diagnosis.

### Roadmap and recommendation

- Required: 3–4 directional phases, first-phase rationale/progress signals, current package connection.
- Optional: later-phase detail, practice commitment, additional evidence.
- Deferred: guaranteed timeline, exhaustive curriculum, product-generated coaching plan.

### Preview/share

- Required: exact golfer order, material blocker review, explicit coach approval, intended recipient/context, accurate share status.
- Optional: personal note and policy-permitted channel choice.
- Deferred: bulk delivery, automated campaigns, public sharing, facility workflow.

## Golfer-facing content requirements

The first roadmap must make available:

1. coach/source identity;
2. golfer goal and personal reason where known;
3. honest starting point with strength;
4. primary pattern and up to two supporting barriers;
5. selected evidence with source and limit where present;
6. 3–4 directional phases;
7. one first-phase recommendation with rationale/signals;
8. one package with purpose, current terms, and external action;
9. ask/wait/decline or appropriate alternative; and
10. correction, access, privacy, and failure expectations.

## Required state content

- New/returning user.
- Complete/incomplete draft.
- Required missing versus optional absent.
- Valid/invalid brand asset.
- Valid/invalid external link.
- Text-only/no evidence.
- No appropriate recommendation.
- Preview blocker and correctable item.
- Share cancelled/pending/failed/duplicate/success.
- External action unavailable.
- Unauthorized/expired access.
- Trial active/ending, explicit paid choice, full-price active.
- Candidate pause/read-only, cancel consequence, retained/deleted status under future policy, resume.
- Long/short content, narrow/wide, zoom/reflow, media alternative.

Status copy says what happened, what did not happen, and what action is available. No error exposes private information or implies an unobserved sale.

## Later lifecycle content outside first product

- lesson chapter/practice prescription;
- progress evidence and setback;
- phase completion and renewal;
- milestone, share, and referral; and
- team administration content.

These remain conceptual requirements for continuity. They are not approved first-build content.

## Length and extreme testing

Test:

- no logo/photo/media/measurement;
- short and long coach/golfer names;
- short and precise long goals;
- package price/terms/currency extremes;
- 3 and 4 phases with long names;
- absent optional evidence and failed media;
- long FAQ and policy text at narrow width;
- translation/language expansion risk where applicable; and
- paused/cancelled state with no prior roadmap.

Do not solve extreme content by truncating material meaning or hiding terms.

## Privacy and policy classification

Potential content includes personal identity, contact context, coaching observations, performance data, media, commercial package details, access/share records, research records, and subscription/account state.

## Future acceptance criteria

- Every required content item traces to an approved screen/state.
- Coach judgments require explicit coach confirmation.
- Optional absence renders intentionally.
- Platform and coach-package prices cannot be confused.
- Preview/share content is consistent.
- Error/account states are accurate and privacy-safe.
- Content is usable narrow/wide, keyboard, zoom/reflow, no-media, reduced motion.
- Real retention, deletion, access, and billing behavior matches approved copy/policy.
