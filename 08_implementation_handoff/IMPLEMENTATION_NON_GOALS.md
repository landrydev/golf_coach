## Boundary

If Aaron later approves a bounded implementation, it should prove the solo instructor's self-serve activation and golfer roadmap loop. It must not become a broad golf business platform, managed service, or technical-architecture exercise.

This document narrows a possible future scope; it does not authorize it.

Aaron has named React/Node.js as the medium for a future local interactive prototype prompt. That is an owner-provided prototype constraint only; it does not select the production stack, authorize current code, or weaken any non-goal below.

## Product non-goals

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]`

- Coaching methodology, diagnosis, curriculum, certification, or autonomous coaching.
- Swing analysis, launch-monitor replacement, video editing/annotation, or performance prediction.
- Booking, scheduling, native payment processing, invoicing, or general CRM.
- Communication platform, public coach marketplace, social network, GPS, simulator, or facility operations.
- Complete academy management, multi-coach roles, or facility reporting in the initial product.
- Custom website builder, custom brand-design service, or bespoke client work.
- Guaranteed coaching, package, renewal, referral, or revenue outcomes.

## Commercial non-goals

- Direct course/facility enterprise sales as the initial motion.
- Custom proposals or quotes.
- Mandatory setup fee or long contract.
- Required sales call or onboarding meeting.
- Founder-built templates/accounts as normal service.
- Concierge roadmap/reporting service.
- Active-golfer pricing as the primary launch model.
- Twelve mandatory full-price months.
- Dependence on a few high-value accounts.
- Paid advertising assumed economical before evidence.

## Candidate first-implementation exclusions

- Full public marketing site beyond the minimum acquisition explanation needed for the tested path.
- Complete coach dashboard, analytics, CRM pipeline, or revenue attribution product.
- Native transaction, billing implementation details beyond separately approved minimum subscription behavior.
- Real-time integrations with booking, payment, video, launch, or communication products.
- Broad media library or raw-data ingestion.
- Migration, import, or data-cleanup service.
- Living journey, renewal, and referral unless explicitly added by a later approved scope decision.
- Annual billing, discounts, referral rewards, or team tier.
- Facility branding/permissions/administration.
- Automated reminders, campaigns, or lead generation.
- Production technical stack, vendor, architecture, hosting, authentication, storage, payments, AI model, or infrastructure choices in pre-code documents. The owner-requested local React/Node prototype medium is the only recorded exception and carries no production precedent.

## Not excluded from current design work

The pre-code design program must still specify and test:

- landing promise, sample, price, FAQ, and signup;
- coach identity/basic branding;
- package and existing next-action setup;
- golfer/assessment/phases;
- preview/share/success/minimal return;
- golfer roadmap/package decision;
- required, optional, error, unauthorized, and responsive states;
- trial/paid/cancel/pause/resume concepts; and
- privacy, access, retention, support, and accessibility implications.

Designing a state does not authorize its implementation.

## Unresolved delivery boundary

Real subscription, authentication, sharing, media, retention, and external-link behavior may eventually require technical choices. Those choices occur only after design approval and within a separately authorized implementation phase. They cannot be selected now to “complete” documentation.

## Scope-change rule

A proposed expansion requires:

1. observed user/business blockage;
2. evidence that an existing link, tool, or narrower flow cannot solve it;
3. impact on activation, support, price, privacy, accessibility, and economics;
4. explicit source/decision update;
5. revised design/prototype/acceptance artifacts; and
6. Aaron approval before implementation.

One facility request, competitor feature, or technical convenience is insufficient.
