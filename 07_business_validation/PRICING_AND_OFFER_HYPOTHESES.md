## Pricing principles

- One understandable initial plan.
- Price visible before signup or payment.
- No custom quote, required call, mandatory setup fee, or long contract.
- Easy self-serve purchase and cancellation.
- Included use encourages roadmap sharing rather than penalizing it.
- Package supports solo instructors without relying on later team revenue.
- Price claims never imply proven ROI or willingness to pay.
- Seasonality is handled openly rather than hidden in an annual commitment.

## Included-usage recommendation

### Why

- The product needs instructors to create and share, not ration usage.
- A golfer count adds tracking and price anxiety before variable cost is known.
- One instructor seat is already a clear packaging boundary.
- A usage allowance can make a simple low-cost offer look like a facility system.

## Seasonality options

| Option | Simplicity | Data continuity | Revenue predictability | Main adoption/churn issue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cancel and later reactivate | High | Depends on retention policy | Low | Reconfiguration or lost context may prevent return |
| Seasonal pause | Moderate | High if read-only | Some pause revenue | Another price/policy to understand |
| Reduced-cost read-only | Moderate | High | Some off-season revenue | May feel like paying for inactivity |
| Annual billing with discount | Moderate | High | Higher | Can deter seasonal instructors and hide churn |
| Monthly + optional annual | Lower initially | High | Flexible | Too much launch packaging before evidence |

## Recommended seasonality policy

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`

- Full service month-to-month at **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $75/month**.
- Cancel anytime.
- Optional seasonal pause at **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $15/month** for up to six consecutive months.
- Pause preserves account, coach identity, package setup, and prior roadmaps in read-only form.
- One clear action resumes full service.
- Defer annual billing until retention and seasonal behavior are observed.

### Rationale

- Gives outdoor instructors a clear six-to-eight-month usage model.
- Reduces reconfiguration and data-loss fear.
- Creates a measurable pause and reactivation cohort.
- Does not force year-round instructors into a special path.
- Preserves customer control and avoids using an annual contract to hide churn.

### Unresolved policy questions

- Whether existing golfer links remain accessible during pause.
- Exact data retention and deletion after cancel versus pause.
- Reminder timing and consent.
- Taxes, billing timing, refunds, failed payment, and proration.
- Pause eligibility, maximum duration, and abuse handling.
- Direct cost of retained data/media.
- Whether the pause fee itself reduces adoption or reactivation.

These require Aaron review, real behavior, and qualified policy/legal input before live use.

## Pricing research sequence

1. Establish recent problem frequency and current cost/workaround.
2. Test unaided product/sample comprehension before price.
3. Show the exact plan and working price; record comprehension first.
4. Ask what business event would justify payment and what would cause cancellation.
5. Compare inclusion models without implying a final choice.
6. In authorized beta, make a real paid offer and record accept/refuse/condition/counteroffer.
7. Measure continued payment, repeat use, pause/cancel reason, and support—not only initial conversion.
8. Observe next-season reactivation before claiming seasonality fit.

Record willingness-to-pay statements as opinion; only real payment behavior is commercial evidence.

## Objections and honest product answers

| Objection | Answer to test | Do not claim |
|---|---|---|
| “I do not have enough golfers.” | Show who the product fits and simple cancellation. | Guaranteed break-even. |
| “A template will be generic.” | Show coach-owned inputs and realistic sample. | Infinite customization. |
| “I am seasonal.” | Explain cancel/pause/resume and what remains available. | Twelve paid months. |
| “I need help.” | Provide product guidance and log reactive support. | Concierge onboarding included. |
| “My current tools do this.” | Compare the full decision story; accept non-fit. | Misrepresented competitors. |
| “Why unlimited?” | Encourage genuine use; disclose future reasonable-use policy if evidence requires. | Unlimited high-cost media regardless of real costs. |
| “Will this make me more money?” | Explain intended package clarity and required evidence. | Revenue guarantee or fabricated ROI. |

## Discount and incentive rules

- No hidden discount or fictional crossed-out anchor.
- No countdown, limited seats, or “founder deal” required to secure learning.
- No annual discount at initial launch recommendation.
- If a future annual option is tested, show total charge, effective monthly amount, renewal, cancellation, refund, and comparison truthfully.
- Referral incentives, if ever tested, must follow real value and cannot require golfer-data sharing.

## Stop or change triggers

Change the offer if:

- qualified activated instructors consistently refuse the working price;
- real use is one-time and does not support recurring payment;
- pause creates more confusion/friction than cancellation/reactivation;
- unlimited ordinary use creates material unsustainable cost;
- trial structure prevents real activation opportunities;
- support/custom work is required to produce credible results; or
- the solo plan depends on a later facility tier to be viable.
