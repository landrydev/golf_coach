## Purpose

Learn from real instructor and golfer situations without turning founder assistance into the long-term offer. This plan replaces the former founder-led paid-pilot/commercial-service model.

Two distinct stages are required:

1. **Assisted beta:** founder help is permitted, bounded, and measured to expose missing product structure.
2. **True self-serve beta:** after design-gate approval and a separately authorized limited implementation, qualified instructors activate with no scheduled onboarding and only logged reactive support.

## Non-negotiable separation

| Behavior | Assisted beta | True self-serve beta |
|---|---|---|
| Founder explains concept live | Permitted for research; record | Not before activation |
| Scheduled onboarding | Permitted only as explicit intervention | Not permitted |
| Founder configures account/brand | Intervention, never activation proof | Not permitted |
| Founder writes/corrects roadmap | Intervention; output excluded from human-free metric | Not permitted before share |
| Reactive support | Logged | Logged and counted in economics |
| Real roadmap share | Permitted after policy/consent | Required for activation |
| Actual price response | May be explored under approved offer | Required for paid evidence |
| Counts as self-serve evidence | No | Yes, within cohort limits |

## Assisted beta

### Sample

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]` Recruit 5–8 core solo instructors across multiple Canadian contexts, with a mix of seasonal and year-round instruction and at least one plausible assessment opportunity during the study window.

Do not require facility ownership, multiple coaches, a specific tool, custom implementation, or local-market location.

### Learning offer

Use the candidate Solo plan structure and **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $75/month** value frame where an actual offer is authorized. Do not charge the superseded CAD $750 service pilot or promise setup consulting as the product.

If research participation is compensated or the beta is free, state that separately; it cannot validate willingness to pay.

### Phases

1. Verify current workflow/baseline and consent.
2. Let the instructor attempt the standard path first.
3. Intervene only when needed; log trigger, work, time, and avoidability.
4. Share real roadmaps only under approved access/consent process.
5. Record golfer comprehension/trust and package outcomes.
6. Observe repeat use and likely recurring-value moments.
7. Conduct a price/continuation decision under the approved offer.
8. Review each intervention as fix, optional help, segment mismatch, or model risk.

### Intervention log

| Field | Required record |
|---|---|
| Trigger | What the instructor attempted and where it failed |
| Assistance type | Explanation, setup, content, correction, policy, technical, other |
| Active minutes | Founder/support time |
| Work performed | Exact help, without private data in this repository |
| Could product prevent it? | Candidate design/content/policy response |
| Recurrence | First instance or repeated across accounts |
| Metric treatment | Excluded from human-free activation; included in assisted effort/support learning |

### Assisted-beta outputs

- Standard content and state gaps.
- Founder intervention distribution.
- Coach effort/authenticity.
- Real share and golfer trust record.
- Commercial outcome signals with limitations.
- Price/refusal/continuation behavior status.
- Policy and incident issues.
- Recommendation for design gate: proceed, revise, pause, or stop.

## True self-serve beta after gate approval

### Sample

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]` At least 15 qualified solo instructors who are new to the product explanation and enter through content, community, partner, referral, search, or direct link—not a required sales demonstration.

### Candidate commercial setup

- Candidate 14-day no-card trial with explicit paid conversion.
- **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $75/month** Solo plan.
- No setup fee, contract, or onboarding meeting.
- Candidate cancel-anytime and seasonal-pause policy shown before purchase.

Exact billing/tax/refund/retention terms must be approved before live use.

### Required behavior

```text
Entry → explanation/sample → signup → setup → create → preview → share
→ real package opportunity → paid choice → repeat/continue/pause/cancel
```

### Support rule

No scheduled walkthrough, custom brand setup, founder-authored content, or proactive rescue before activation. Reactive support is allowed and logged by reason/minutes. Accessibility accommodations are provided and reported separately from avoidable product support.

### Measurement windows

- First session: comprehension, setup, draft, preview, share, time, support.
- Trial window: activation and paid choice.
- First 30/60/90 days: repeat use, MRR, churn, support, package opportunities/outcomes.
- Full season/year where possible: paid months, pause/cancel, reactivation, annual interest, retention.

Do not infer annual retention from a short launch cohort.

## Golfer sample and safeguards

- Adult golfers only until junior safeguards are separately resolved.
- Real golfers must understand the role of coach/product and the commercial recommendation.
- Consent to coaching is not automatic research/product/share consent.
- A golfer may ask, wait, decline, correct, withdraw from research, and use the applicable access/deletion path without affecting coach respect.
- Pressure, misleading evidence, unsafe access, or privacy incident triggers immediate review and may stop the beta.

## Comparison and attribution

Where instructor records allow, predeclare eligible assessment baseline and compare with the intervention period. Report counts, not only percentages. Record season, lead source, package change, coach, price, availability, and other confounders.

The beta cannot establish causality from a simple pre/post difference. Customer-outcome metrics support the value case; SaaS metrics evaluate the company model.

## Stop rules

Stop or pause for:

- consent withdrawal or unsafe data/media handling;
- hidden or misleading commercial terms;
- material golfer pressure or unsupported claim;
- recurring founder-created roadmaps or custom setup;
- normal support incompatible with low-price economics;
- inability to complete real shares without onboarding;
- target segment consistently requiring facility approval/team features; or
- gate/policy prerequisites not met.
