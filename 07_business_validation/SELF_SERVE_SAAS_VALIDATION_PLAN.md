## Purpose

Determine whether the owner-directed self-serve direction is desirable, understandable, usable, trustworthy, and commercially viable for individual Canadian golf instructors—without confusing early founder help with proof of a scalable low-cost SaaS.

## Questions that must be answered

1. Can an instructor understand the value without a live explanation?
2. Can an instructor sign up without help?
3. Can an instructor create the first roadmap without help?
4. Can standardized templates produce something sufficiently personal and coach-owned?
5. Will an instructor share it with a real golfer?
6. Does the golfer understand the plan and package recommendation?
7. Does the experience improve package confidence or conversion without pressure?
8. Will an instructor pay **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] approximately CAD $75 per month**?
9. Will the instructor remain subscribed after the first package opportunity?
10. How does seasonality affect paid months, cancellation, pause, and retention?
11. Will paused or cancelled instructors return next season?
12. Can support and acquisition remain low enough for the price?

No single study can answer all questions. Evidence must match the claim.

## Four validation modes

| Mode | Founder role | What it can establish | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assisted research | Interviews, observation, concept probing | Problem, workflow, language, context, objections | Self-serve activation, payment, retention |
| Assisted beta | Help allowed and logged | Real content/workflow gaps, golfer use, support categories, early outcome signals | Human-free activation or scalable support |
| True self-serve activation | No scheduled onboarding; reactive support logged | Real discovery/signup/create/share behavior and early paid choice | Scaled CAC or full seasonal retention without sufficient cohort/time |
| Scaled self-serve acquisition | Product-led channels and cohorts | Funnel, CAC, payback, referral contribution, retention, seasonality | Causality beyond defined cohorts |

An assisted success may justify a design change. It must never be counted as self-serve proof.

### Stage 3 — non-coded activation prototype

- 5 instructors review authenticity, coach ownership, workflow fit, and support risk.

Proceed only when the golfer outcome remains clear and ethical and standardization does not erase coach authority.

A manual/non-coded learning process may be used only within approved boundaries; it must not be marketed as a finished self-serve product.

### Stage 8 — seasonal and product-led cohorts

Only after activation and trust pass:

- test later team demand without adding team scope prematurely.

Do not assume paid advertising or annual billing is economical.

## Evidence matrix

| Claim | Minimum relevant evidence | Insufficient substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Problem exists | Recent cases/artifacts across core instructors | General enthusiasm |
| Value understood unaided | Accurate retell from unexposed visitors | Founder demo feedback |
| Setup works | Observed unassisted task completion | “Looks easy” opinion |
| Standard output is personal | Coach-authored result + golfer detail retell | Name/logo personalization |
| Activation works | Complete real share without onboarding | Draft or founder-assisted share |
| Golfer value | Comprehension, trust, action in exact version | Visual preference |
| Package effect | Defined real cohorts and limitations | One anecdotal sale |
| Price accepted | Actual paid choice/continuation | 1–10 price rating |
| Support is low | Minutes/reason by normal account | Excluding recurring “beta help” |
| Acquisition is viable | Channel-to-paid CAC/payback cohorts | Cheap clicks or follower count |
| Seasonality is viable | Full-season paid/pause/cancel/reactivation cohort | Annual-intent survey |

## Go / no-go logic for self-service

### Proceed

Proceed when unaided comprehension, activation, golfer trust, actual payment, support, and early retention collectively support the model without violating guardrails.

### Modify

Modify when the core problem and value exist but a bounded message, input, sequence, template, policy, price, trial, or seasonality issue blocks behavior.

### Pause

Pause when required evidence is missing, participant eligibility is weak, privacy/policy is unresolved, or a full seasonal cycle is needed.

### Stop or reconsider the model

Stop when value repeatedly depends on founder explanation, custom setup, bespoke outputs, mandatory onboarding, high support, facility procurement, manipulative sales, or acquisition cost incompatible with the low price.

Do not “pass” self-service by increasing the amount of founder help.

## Highest-risk assumptions

1. Unaided proposition comprehension.
2. Standard template personal relevance.
3. Fast coach-owned first roadmap.
4. Real golfer share and package opportunity.

9. Seasonal retention/reactivation.
