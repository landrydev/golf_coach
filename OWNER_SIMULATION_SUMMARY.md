## Company recommendation

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]`

Help independent instructors sell more lesson packages, renew more golfers, and generate more referrals by converting existing assessment evidence into a personalized, coach-branded development roadmap and living player journey.

The product sits above current coaching methods and tools. It does not initially replace coaching methodology, video analysis, launch monitors, booking, payments, communication, or the instructor's effective workflow.

The long-term commercial experience does not depend on a sales call, custom proposal, custom implementation, onboarding meeting, setup fee, concierge service, bespoke work, or golf-course contract.

## Initial customer

An individual independent golf instructor or teaching professional in Canada who:

- controls or influences lesson-package recommendations;
- conducts assessments or initial evaluations;
- sells multiple lessons to at least some golfers;
- uses observations and possibly video, drills, or measurements;
- wants a more professional and persuasive golfer experience;
- wants to keep current methods/tools;
- is comfortable with simple self-serve software; and
- could compare value with **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] approximately CAD $75/month**.

The instructor does not need to own a facility, run an academy, manage coaches, use a particular tool, or buy consulting. Small teams/facilities are later self-serve expansion candidates.

## Core product loop

```text
Assessment
→ personalized roadmap
→ initial lesson-package purchase
→ visible progress
→ phase completion
→ next-package renewal
→ referral
```

First wedge:

```text
Assessment → personalized roadmap → first coaching phase → lesson-package decision
```

The golfer buys through clarity, personal relevance, evidence, coach credibility, reduced uncertainty, trust, and a natural next step—never manipulation.

## Recommended first product

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`

Enable one instructor to:

1. understand the product, see a realistic sample, and view price/terms;
2. create an account without assistance;
3. enter basic coach identity/branding;
4. define a current lesson package and existing action link;
5. add one golfer and desired outcome;
6. record a concise assessment and barriers;
7. define three or four phases;
8. select the first phase and connect it to the package;
9. preview the exact golfer experience;
10. share the roadmap;
11. receive accurate success/failure status; and
12. return to a minimal recent/create/continue state.

Do not build a complete dashboard, CRM, booking/payment platform, analysis engine, communication product, marketplace, academy system, custom website builder, autonomous coach, or custom-service workflow.

## Recommended activation

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`

> An instructor creates and shares a complete golfer roadmap connected to a current lesson package, without human onboarding.

Candidate usability target: a qualified instructor reaches the share within 30 active minutes in the first session. The target is not validated and must not be marketed as fact.

## Pricing and packaging

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`

One **Solo Instructor** plan at **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $75 per month per instructor**:

- one coach identity and standard branding;
- standardized templates/prompts;
- unlimited roadmaps and active golfer records within ordinary solo use;
- package setup and external next-action links;
- preview and sharing;
- later validated progress/renewal/referral deliverables;
- no mandatory setup fee, custom quote, sales call, or long contract; and
- cancel anytime under an approved future policy.

Do not begin with active-golfer pricing. Add a transparent reasonable-use/media allowance only if actual cost/use evidence requires it.

## Seasonality recommendation

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`

- Full month-to-month service at **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $75/month**.
- Cancel anytime.
- Optional read-only seasonal pause at **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $15/month** for up to six months.
- Preserve identity, package, and roadmaps; resume with one clear action.
- Defer annual billing until actual retention/seasonality is known.

This balances simplicity, data continuity, reactivation friction, and revenue predictability without forcing a twelve-month contract. Exact pause price, data/link behavior, billing, retention, deletion, and reminders remain unresolved.

## Metrics recommendation

### Product activation metric

Percentage of qualified instructors who create and share a complete package-connected roadmap without human onboarding.

### Company North Star

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]` Trailing-twelve-month seasonalized recurring subscription revenue from activated instructors. Before one year, report cohort MRR, activated paid instructors, and full-price paid months—not peak MRR × 12.

### Customer-success metrics retained

- assessment-to-package conversion;
- revenue per eligible assessed golfer;
- lessons purchased per golfer;
- phase/package renewal; and
- instructor/golfer referral.

Revenue per assessed golfer is no longer the company's primary North Star.

### Working economic thresholds

All are `[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`:

- 8 full-price paid months/customer/year working case;
- at least 80% gross margin before R&D/overhead;
- no more than 10 median support minutes/account/month after activation;
- at least 80% of activated instructors with no human onboarding;
- at least 40% qualified signup-to-activation;
- at least 20% qualified trial-to-paid;
- no more than 4 full-price paid months CAC payback; and
- at least 40% eligible next-season reactivation.

These are planning thresholds, not benchmarks or forecasts.

## Validation recommendation

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`

1. 12-instructor discovery: 10 core solo + 2 team boundary.
2. 12-golfer discovery.
3. Unaided acquisition/sample/price comprehension.
4. Non-coded activation prototype with 8 unexposed instructors.
5. Golfer prototype with 10 golfers + 5 authenticity reviews.
6. Assisted beta with 5–8 instructors, every intervention logged.
7. Design-gate decision.
8. After separate code authorization, true self-serve beta with at least 15 instructors and reactive support only.
9. Product-led and full-season cohorts only after activation/trust pass.

Assisted research and beta can find problems. They cannot prove human-free activation or scalable economics.

## Highest-risk assumptions

1. The proposition is clear without live explanation.
2. A standard template can remain genuinely personal and coach-owned.
3. The first roadmap is fast enough for normal workflow.
4. Instructors share with real golfers.
5. Golfers understand and gain package confidence without pressure.
6. The product influences package, renewal, or referral outcomes.
7. **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $75/month** earns real payment and continuation.
8. Acquisition and support fit low-price economics.
9. Seasonal pause/cancellation produces acceptable paid months and reactivation.
10. Recurring value exists beyond the first share.
