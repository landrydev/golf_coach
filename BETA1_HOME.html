How this Beta behaves

  1. Everything is synthetic and stays on your computer.
  2. Each named scenario has a separate synthetic instructor account.
  3. You can edit, publish, upload, import, replace, revoke, and respond during the session.
  4. Closing the server resets the local in-memory scenario data, giving you a clean replay next time.
  5. This is for product experience and functional review—not public launch, payment, or real customer data.
