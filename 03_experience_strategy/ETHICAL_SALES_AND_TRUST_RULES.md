## Ethical standard

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]`

> The instructor should subscribe because the product's value, effort, price, and terms are clear. The golfer should buy because the coach's value and next step are easier to understand. Neither decision should depend on interface pressure.

Commercial effectiveness and autonomy are not opposing goals. If a design requires ambiguity, fear, hidden labor, or exaggerated evidence to convert, it fails.

## SaaS acquisition and subscription rules

1. Identify the intended solo instructor and actual product job plainly.
2. Show a realistic output and the input effort needed; do not present founder-created polish as normal self-serve output.
3. State compatibility and non-replacement boundaries before signup.
4. Present **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $75/month per instructor**, included use, renewal cadence, trial behavior, taxes/currency context when known, cancellation, and candidate pause terms before payment.
5. Do not require a call, custom quote, setup fee, or annual commitment in the recommended initial path.
6. A trial must not convert through obscured consent; the candidate no-card trial requires an explicit paid choice.
7. Cancellation and pause must not be harder to find or complete than ordinary account continuation.
8. Explain data access, retention, sharing-link behavior, deletion, and reactivation truthfully once policy is approved.
9. Do not use fake countdowns, false scarcity, invented adoption claims, hidden discounts, or savings claims without a valid comparison.
10. Record founder assistance and support honestly; do not market an assisted result as unassisted product value.

## Golfer package-decision rules

1. Acknowledge the golfer's goal before technical findings or price.
2. Explain the starting point, barriers, coach reasoning, roadmap, and first-phase purpose before the package.
3. Present one clear recommendation, while keeping ask, wait, decline, reassessment, and appropriate independent-practice paths discoverable.
4. Show package price, currency, inclusions, cadence, material terms, evaluation, and phase-completion behavior before action.
5. Make clear when an external purchase, booking, or contact link leaves the roadmap.
6. Do not use urgency, scarcity, guilt, shame, fear of losing progress, default add-ons, or repeated intrusive prompts.
7. Do not imply that purchase is required to preserve coach respect or prior progress.
8. A non-purchase, pause, or independent-practice recommendation can be correct.

## Recommendation standard

A package recommendation appears only when the instructor confirms that:

- it connects to the golfer's stated goal;
- the starting evidence supports the priority;
- the package is an appropriate container for the first phase;
- expected golfer commitment is realistic;
- price and terms are current;
- no outcome is guaranteed; and
- the instructor would still make the recommendation without a conversion target.

If a condition fails, show a question, reassessment, independent-practice, referral-to-another-service, or no-recommendation state.

## Claim and evidence rules

- Label material claims as coach-observed, measured, golfer-reported, on-course recorded, early indication, or not yet evidenced.
- State relevant context and limitation for measurements.
- Explain what evidence supports and what it does not prove.
- Do not cherry-pick a best swing, round, or isolated number as typical.
- Distinguish demonstrated once, repeated in practice, reliable under variation, course transfer, and scoring result.
- Do not guarantee handicap, score, injury, swing, conversion, renewal, referral, revenue, or timeline outcomes.
- Roadmap phases remain directional.
- Correct materially misleading claims promptly under the future approved process.

## Setback and uncertainty pattern

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`

> **What happened:** name it without judgment.  
> **What we know:** state supported observations.  
> **What remains uncertain:** state limits.  
> **What remains stable:** identify retained learning honestly.  
> **What happens next:** give one manageable action and reassessment point.

No renewal recommendation appears merely because performance declined.

## Phase-completion and renewal rules

1. Show original purpose and starting condition.
2. Show work completed, representative evidence, reliability, and limitations.
3. Recognize golfer contribution without blaming effort for results.
4. State whether the phase is complete, partial, paused, or revised.
5. Explain remaining opportunity before another package.
6. Make the next phase distinct and evidence-based.
7. Show continue, ask, pause, or independent-practice choices.
8. Never imply that all progress disappears without immediate renewal.

## Milestone, sharing, and referral rules

- Milestones are coach-confirmed, specific, infrequent, and meaningful.
- The private milestone exists before sharing.
- The golfer selects what may leave the private experience and sees a complete preview.
- Sharing and referral are separate choices.
- No automatic posting, preselected audience, or public-by-default link.
- Coach branding is restrained; the golfer's achievement leads.
- Revocation, link behavior, retention, download, onward sharing, and consent require qualified policy review.
- Never expose another golfer, unapproved lesson detail, hidden metadata, contact detail, or precise location by default.

## Privacy and consent constraints

- Initial research and beta scope is adults only unless separately reviewed.
- Identifiable raw research or customer data is not stored in this planning repository.
- Access is private by default; unauthorized states reveal no golfer detail.
- Collect only information required for the approved experience.
- Coaching consent does not automatically include research, testing, marketing, or sharing.
- Coach/golfer access, correction, deletion, retention, media consent, research consent, share consent, withdrawal, paused-account access, and cancelled-account treatment must be defined before live use.
- Junior, guardian, safeguarding, and team-account requirements remain outside initial scope.

`[REAL-WORLD VALIDATION REQUIRED]` Obtain qualified legal/privacy advice for the actual Canadian jurisdictions and operating process before real identifiable data, media, live sharing, billing, pause, or retention.

## Accessibility is a trust rule

- Product explanation, price, terms, required inputs, errors, and cancellation must be accessible.
- Golfer recommendation basis, alternatives, privacy, and errors must remain available to keyboard and assistive-technology users.
- Never communicate evidence quality, state, risk, or selected share content through color alone.
- Video requires equivalent text context; speech requires captions/transcript where applicable.
- Motion is optional and never the only comparison mechanism.
- No time limit should pressure signup, purchase, renewal, or sharing.
- Error recovery must protect privacy and preserve a legitimate alternative.

## Ethical review checklist

- [ ] Website outcome and price claims match evidence and labels.
- [ ] Normal self-serve effort is not represented by an assisted ideal.
- [ ] Trial, recurring charge, cancel, pause, access, and resume are understandable.
- [ ] Golfer value appears before package action.
- [ ] Coach ownership and evidence limits are explicit.
- [ ] Package purpose, price, inclusions, terms, and alternatives are visible.
- [ ] No urgency, scarcity, guilt, shame, fear, hidden alternative, or guaranteed outcome.
- [ ] Setbacks and incomplete progress remain visible.
- [ ] Renewal follows evidence-based review.
- [ ] Private/non-sharing and cancel/pause paths are genuine.
- [ ] Accessibility preserves information and control.
- [ ] Qualified policy review occurs before live data use.

One failure on manipulation, material claim accuracy, hidden terms, privacy, or access blocks the affected artifact.
