## Dual experience thesis

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]`

The instructor should feel: **“I understand what this will help me sell, I can keep my current tools, and I can create something credible without needing the founder.”**

The golfer should feel: **“My coach listened carefully, understands my game and goal, has a credible plan, and has made the next coaching decision easy to understand.”**

The experience succeeds only when both statements are true. A polished golfer output that depends on custom service is not the self-serve product; a fast authoring tool that produces a generic or pressuring output does not deliver the product thesis.

## Instructor experience promise

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]`

From the first acquisition touch, the product explains its commercial job, shows a realistic result, states the work required, preserves existing tools and methodology, presents the price plainly, and leads to a real first roadmap through standard guidance.

The instructor experience should feel like calm utility:

- direct rather than enterprise-oriented;
- guided rather than dependent;
- standardized rather than bespoke;
- practical rather than feature-heavy;
- transparent about price, effort, limits, and support; and
- confident without implying guaranteed revenue.

## Golfer experience promise

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]`

The golfer receives a private, coach-branded performance portfolio and development roadmap. The coach remains visibly responsible for observations, priorities, interpretations, and recommendations. Evidence reduces uncertainty; it never guarantees a result.

The experience earns sales by making legitimate structured coaching easier to understand than a verbal suggestion, disconnected clips, or a package of hours.

## What “personal” means

Personal means the golfer-facing result could not be truthfully sent to a different golfer without material rewriting. It reflects goal, reason, context, strength, performance pattern, coach rationale, selected evidence and limits, practice reality, and—later—history or progress.

Name, photograph, logo, and accent color are identity signals, not sufficient personalization.

## What “professional quality” means

Professional quality comes from:

- memory of the relationship;
- specificity and coach-owned reasoning;
- restraint and hierarchy;
- curated evidence with limits;
- continuity across phases;
- clear next questions and actions;
- accurate states and error recovery; and
- accessible, responsive presentation.

It does not require a premium price, luxury language, custom design engagement, dense analytics, or an enterprise account.

## What the experience must not resemble

- An enterprise sales funnel or gated demo site.
- A vague SaaS feature grid with no realistic output.
- A custom agency intake and implementation project.
- A generic dashboard before the instructor creates value.
- A launch-monitor report, video folder, or technical data dump.
- A hard-selling golfer purchase page.
- A clinical diagnosis that makes the golfer feel broken.
- A templated report personalized only by name and color.
- A coachless automated recommendation.

## How the experience earns business outcomes

1. The instructor understands the product and required effort.
2. The sample makes the output credible.
3. Standard setup produces a coach-owned first roadmap.
4. The golfer recognizes the goal and context.
5. Coach expertise appears through prioritization and explanation.
6. A phased path reduces uncertainty without promising outcomes.
7. The package becomes the practical container for the first phase.
8. Price, terms, action, and alternatives are clear.
9. Later progress and completion make renewal and referral worth considering.

The product must not manufacture a need; it makes a legitimate recommendation easier to evaluate.

## Treatment of uncertainty and setbacks

- Roadmaps are directional and may change.
- Demonstrated once, repeated in practice, reliable under variation, transferred to the course, and reflected in scoring are distinct.
- A poor round can coexist with progress; neither should be hidden.
- When evidence is weak, state what is known, unknown, stable, changing, and when reassessment occurs.
- Independent practice, a pause, or no new package may be the correct recommendation.

## Observable signs the vision is present

- An unexposed instructor accurately explains the product, required inputs, compatibility, price, and first action from the acquisition experience.
- A qualified instructor completes the first share without human onboarding and can identify what to do on return.
- A golfer accurately retells the goal, starting point, first-phase purpose, package purpose, and alternatives.
- The instructor says the roadmap represents their judgment within acceptable measured effort.
- Personal relevance is substantive, not cosmetic.
- Limited-data, error, setback, cancel, and pause states preserve trust.
