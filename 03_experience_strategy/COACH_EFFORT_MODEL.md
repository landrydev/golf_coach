## Effort thesis

The product may ask the instructor to make and confirm high-value coaching judgments. It must not ask for a bespoke report, custom implementation project, onboarding meeting, or recurring concierge process.

Existing goals, notes, clips, measurements, drills, package terms, brand elements, and purchase links should be reused. New work should be limited to selecting, structuring, explaining briefly, previewing, and approving.

Effort and support are product outcomes. A completed roadmap created through founder labor is not evidence that the self-serve workflow works.

## Responsibility rule

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]`

The instructor must author or explicitly approve:

- starting assessment and evidence interpretation;
- barriers and priority;
- phase names, purposes, and sequence;
- current or recommended first phase;
- package recommendation and fit;
- progress, setback, milestone, and completion claims; and
- any recommendation to continue, pause, or practise independently.

Standard prompts may organize input but may not silently make coaching judgments.

## First-session effort model

### Understand and begin

- Review promise, sample, compatibility, work required, price, trial, and privacy expectations.
- Required human assistance: none.
- `[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]` Candidate comprehension-to-signup target: 5 active minutes or less for a qualified instructor who chooses to continue.

### Basic coach setup — once

- Required: display name/identity, contact route, one package, and existing next-action link.
- Optional: logo, photo, brand accent, short philosophy, extended package detail.
- Deferred: custom visual exploration, facility configuration, multiple coaches, advanced templates.
- `[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]` Candidate median active effort: 10 minutes or less for minimum credible setup.

### First assessment roadmap

Minimum input:

1. confirm golfer goal and reason;
2. state one or two strengths;
3. state the primary pattern and up to two supporting barriers;
4. optionally select up to three evidence items and explain limits;
5. name three or four directional phases;
6. identify first phase, rationale, and progress signals;
7. select the current package and verify its terms/link; and
8. preview and approve the complete golfer experience.

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]` Candidate median active effort: 15 minutes or less after minimum setup, with a candidate first-session signup-to-share total of 30 active minutes or less. Treat both as usability hypotheses, not promises.

## Minimum-effort workflow

1. **Prepare:** show the short readiness list before signup or setup.
2. **Capture once:** use facts already needed for coaching.
3. **Select:** choose the smallest evidence set supporting the story.
4. **Structure:** use standard prompts and phase patterns.
5. **Confirm:** coach reviews goal, priority, phase, and package fit.
6. **Preview:** coach sees the exact golfer-facing order, terms, and states.
7. **Approve and share:** nothing client-facing is released without confirmation.
8. **Reuse:** identity, package, goal, and roadmap context carry forward.
9. **Update by exception:** add later content only when it changes what the golfer needs to understand.

This is an experience workflow, not an automation or architecture choice.

## Support model

### Product help expected

- Readiness checklist and estimated task scope labelled as a hypothesis.
- Small input examples and explanations of why each field matters.
- Required, optional, and deferred labels.
- Save/return expectations and non-destructive editing.
- Preview, validation summary, and clear corrections.
- Empty and error states with a direct recovery action.
- Plain FAQ and concise reactive support route.

### Normal service excludes

- Scheduled onboarding.
- Founder-created roadmaps.
- Custom branding or template projects.
- Package consulting.
- Data cleanup or migration.
- Facility configuration.
- Ongoing performance or conversion reporting performed by a person.

### Support threshold

`[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]` Target median normal support after activation of no more than 10 minutes per active account per month, and at least 80% of activated instructors receiving value without human onboarding.

Assisted research and beta labor must be separately reported by reason and must not be used to make normal support look lower.

## Content restraint limits

- One primary goal and at most two supporting outcomes.
- One or two strengths.
- One primary pattern and up to two supporting barriers.
- Zero to three selected evidence items in the first story.
- Three or four phases; future phases receive less detail.
- One recommended first phase and package.
- One primary next action plus ask/wait/decline.
- No optional identity, media, measurement, or later-lifecycle field blocks first value.

## Measurement protocol

`[REAL-WORLD VALIDATION REQUIRED]` For every timed task:

- start at the relevant acquisition/setup/authoring entry and stop at the declared outcome;
- record active and elapsed time separately;
- record all moderator, founder, support, and peer assistance;
- record errors, corrections, skipped optional fields, reused inputs, abandonment, and recovery;
- rate content accuracy and authentic coach ownership separately from speed;
- distinguish the first learning attempt from later attempts; and
- report median, range, and percentiles only when the sample supports them.

## Effort failure responses

| Failure | First response | Do not do |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor cannot tell what to prepare | Improve readiness and sample content. | Add a required discovery call. |
| Setup becomes a branding project | Reduce to name, contact, and optional identity tokens. | Sell custom design. |
| Long roadmap narratives | Use short coach judgments and examples. | Hide founder writing as automation. |
| Too much evidence selection | Default to none or one representative item. | Require every clip or metric. |
| Package entry repeats | Reuse package facts with case-specific fit confirmation. | Infer recommendation. |
| Support repeats across users | Fix the product explanation or state. | Normalize recurring onboarding. |
| Later journey becomes a feed | Update only meaningful priority/evidence changes. | Force activity cadence. |
| Coach skips review | Prevent sharing until explicit approval. | Treat inactivity as consent. |
