## Journey thesis

`[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]`

The business contains two connected journeys:

1. the SaaS journey that helps an instructor discover, activate, pay, continue, pause/cancel, reactivate, and refer; and
2. the golfer journey that moves from assessment to roadmap, package, progress, renewal, and referral.

The instructor journey must not require founder service. The golfer journey must not use pressure. A shareable roadmap connected to a real package is the bridge between them.

## Bridge between journeys

```text
Instructor completes standard setup
→ instructor authors and previews a credible roadmap
→ roadmap is shared with the intended golfer
→ golfer understands the first phase and package
→ golfer chooses buy / ask / wait / decline
→ instructor sees a real commercial outcome
→ product supports repeat or lifecycle use
```

The activation event occurs at share, not at package purchase. Package purchase is an instructor customer-success outcome that may follow; it must not be forced to define product usability.

## Critical decision branches

- **Instructor is not ready:** save draft and show the exact missing requirement; do not schedule mandatory onboarding.
- **No logo or media:** proceed with strong text and coach identity.
- **No package yet:** defer sharing or use a contact/reassessment state; do not invent an offer.
- **No recommendation appropriate:** show plan value and a question, reassessment, pause, or independent-practice route.
- **External link unavailable:** preserve the roadmap and show a direct contact path.
- **Golfer waits or declines:** record only appropriate outcome context and preserve dignity/access under the future policy.
- **Instructor enters off-season:** offer clear continue, pause, or cancel choices without fear language.
- **Instructor returns:** restore configuration and lead to the next real roadmap, not a sales call.

## Validation sequence

1. Recent instructor and golfer journeys without the concept.
2. Unaided acquisition and sample comprehension.
3. Unassisted activation prototype.
4. Golfer roadmap comprehension and trust.
5. Assisted beta with intervention logging.
6. True self-serve beta only after design approval.
7. Longitudinal retention, seasonality, renewal, and referral.
