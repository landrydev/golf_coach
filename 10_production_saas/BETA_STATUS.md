# Beta Status

**Canonical candidate:** Roadmap Beta Recovery Build `0.1.0`  
**Release class:** invite-only controlled beta candidate  
**Public/paid release:** not authorized  
**Customer evidence:** not yet collected

## Implemented

- invite-code registration and login;
- password hashing, signed sessions, CSRF protection, and tenant isolation;
- coach profile and reusable external lesson packages;
- golfer records and guided roadmap authoring;
- goals, assessment, strengths, barriers, current priority, evidence limits;
- three or four coach-authored phases;
- lessons, practice assignments, selected evidence, and phase reviews;
- exact coach preview;
- private publication, replacement, and revocation;
- mobile-first golfer roadmap;
- bounded golfer responses: ready, ask, wait, or decline;
- JSON export and persistent local storage;
- automated end-to-end verification.

## Intentionally excluded from this beta

- live billing or Stripe;
- native booking or lesson-package payment;
- email, SMS, or messaging;
- media uploads or swing analysis;
- launch-monitor file import;
- AI diagnosis or roadmap generation;
- junior golfers;
- teams, academies, or facility administration;
- public discovery or public profiles.

## Beta success criteria

The beta earns continuation only when observed use supports all of the following:

1. an unexposed qualified instructor understands the product without a sales call;
2. the instructor creates and publishes a credible first roadmap without scheduled onboarding;
3. median instructor creation time is acceptable for the real assessment workflow;
4. the golfer can accurately retell the goal, current priority, first phase, and next action;
5. the roadmap feels personal and coach-owned rather than generic;
6. instructors share it with real adult golfers;
7. at least some instructors make a real paid or continuation choice at an explicitly tested offer; and
8. support effort remains compatible with a low-price self-serve business.

## Current blockers to opening the beta

- choose the exact beta instructors and adult golfers;
- approve the beta consent/privacy notice and data-retention procedure;
- choose the host, persistent disk, backup owner, HTTPS origin, and support contact;
- run a final manual browser/accessibility review on the deployed exact candidate;
- record the beta offer, whether it is free or paid, and the stop conditions.
