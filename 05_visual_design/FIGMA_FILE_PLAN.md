## Acquisition frame inventory

Required variants: rich and static/text sample; direct-outreach and generic awareness entry; narrow/wide price; operable FAQ states; no-call CTA; honest non-fit/later-team explanation.

## Activation frame inventory

Canonical path: signup; coach identity; package/external action; first golfer/goal; assessment/optional evidence; phases; first-phase/package connection; golfer preview; share review; success; minimal return state.

The [12-step specification](../04_wireframes/SELF_SERVE_INSTRUCTOR_ACTIVATION.md) separates marketing promise and sample before signup. Frames may group content only when all step questions and states remain testable.

Required variants:

- new/existing account and recovery;
- no logo, invalid asset, safe brand default;
- package incomplete, no formal package, invalid link, contact alternative;
- goal missing and optional context absent;
- text-only assessment and evidence unavailable;
- phases incomplete/revised and no appropriate recommendation;
- preview optional omission/material blocker;
- share cancelled/failed/pending/duplicate/success;
- empty/incomplete/complete return;
- trial, explicit paid choice, active, pause, cancel, retained/read-only, resume.
