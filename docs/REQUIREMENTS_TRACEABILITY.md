## Evidence boundary

This matrix maps every bounded `V1-*` and cross-cutting `X-*` requirement to the exact version-13 code and verification responsibility. It distinguishes locally exercised behavior and limited owner-only hosted evidence from work that still needs authenticated hosting, a human or real-user assessment, an owner decision, provider configuration, or qualified external review.

The [version 13 release record](release-evidence/ROADMAP-SITES-V13-2026-08-09.md) binds this matrix to the exact pushed commit, checked archive, provider package, saved version, deployment, environment revision, and runtime release identity. Its hosted evidence is limited to successful owner-only deployment and four fresh signed-out `401` denials. Version 13 retains the nonce CSP and scoped golfer-response idempotency, and adds application-wide write containment, bounded draft/session recovery, stricter response/CAS/revision boundaries, native billing recovery, account share revoke/reissue/replacement receipts, and migration `0010_steep_hemingway`. The supported Browser list was empty, so no signed-in hosted browser was available for CSP, hydration, interaction, accessibility, write/freeze, or recovery retesting; `SEC-002` is **REMEDIATED — HOSTED RETEST PENDING**, not closed. The exact-v13 local recovery and capacity exercises remain synthetic, not hosted/provider evidence. Version-12, version-11, and version-9 records remain historical predecessor evidence. No local result becomes exact-v13 hosted, manual accessibility, provider-native recovery, rollback/forward-fix, RPO/RTO, real-user, policy, or acceptance evidence.

### Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `EVIDENCED LOCAL` | Specific current-candidate code and direct automated local or production-bundle evidence cover the stated technical behavior. It does not mean hosted, manual, real-user, legal, or owner acceptance evidence. |
| `PARTIAL — LOCAL` | Direct local evidence covers part of the row, while one or more material technical, manual, hosted, or real-world dimensions remain unproved. |
| `PARTIAL — LOCAL SYNTHETIC` | A deterministic local exercise covers synthetic state only. It is not provider-native, hosted, customer-data, RPO, RTO, or operator-readiness evidence. |
| `OWNER/EXTERNAL DEPENDENCY` | An exact owner decision, credential/configuration, provider operation, qualified review, public entry point, real-user exercise, or release acceptance is required. Local implementation may still be linked. |
| `EXCLUDED` | Outside the bounded V1 unless a later explicit scope decision supersedes it. |

An `EVIDENCED LOCAL` row can still carry an explicit hosted or human limitation. Hosted evidence is narrow and explicitly stated; no row is fully `EVIDENCED HOSTED`.

### Label discipline

- `[SUPPORTED BY BUSINESS PLAN V2]` is used only for facts established by the authoritative V2 business source.
- `[SIMULATED OWNER DECISION — REQUIRES AARON REVIEW]` remains attached to unapproved candidate scope, packaging, policy, or experience decisions inherited from planning documents.
- `[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION]` remains attached to any planning price or pause amount.
- `[UNVALIDATED BUSINESS ASSUMPTION]` identifies a business belief that implementation cannot validate by itself.
- `[REAL-WORLD VALIDATION REQUIRED]` identifies a claim that needs qualified review or observed real-world evidence.
