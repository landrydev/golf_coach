# Evidence and Claim Log

**CURRENT STATUS:** Structure revised for Business Plan V2; no primary participant evidence entered  
**Purpose:** Prevent owner direction, simulation, expectation, and research from being blended.

## Evidence-handling rules

1. One row represents one discrete claim or observation.
2. Record the exact study stage: discovery, message test, activation prototype, golfer prototype, assisted beta, true self-serve beta, or scaled acquisition.
3. Record whether the behavior was unassisted, reactively supported, or actively assisted.
4. Preserve negative, contradictory, abandoned, and incomplete behavior.
5. A participant quote supports only the proposition it actually addresses.
6. Stated willingness to pay is not payment evidence.
7. An assisted roadmap is not human-free activation.
8. A founder-recruited participant is not evidence that the acquisition channel scales.
9. Synthetic personas, simulated owner choices, and planning targets never enter as participant evidence.
10. Do not store identifiable participant or golfer records in this repository.

## Evidence quality levels

| Level | Description | Example use |
|---|---|---|
| E0 | Simulation or untested assumption | Planning only; never described as research |
| E1 | Participant opinion or recall without artifact | Language and hypothesis formation |
| E2 | Recent behavior with artifact or reliable trace | Problem and workflow support |
| E3 | Observed task behavior on a versioned concept/prototype | Comprehension and usability decisions |
| E4 | Real commercial or product behavior in a defined cohort | Payment, activation, support, retention, and outcome decisions |
| E5 | Repeated multi-cohort or longitudinal behavior | Stronger operating evidence, still bounded by context |

## Synthesis decision standard

Do not use majority language without numerator, denominator, eligibility, stage, and assistance status. A supported hypothesis must have the right kind of evidence for the claim. One severe ethical, privacy, accessibility, or misleading-claim failure can override favorable preference data.

## Completion criteria for real synthesis

- All sessions have discrete evidence entries.
- Unassisted and assisted behavior is separated.
- Price opinion and payment are separated.
- Seasonal and non-seasonal cancellation are separated.
- Each conclusion cites evidence IDs and counterevidence.
- Missingness and cohort limitations are explicit.
- Owner decisions are recorded in [DECISION_LOG.md](../DECISION_LOG.md), not backfilled into evidence.
