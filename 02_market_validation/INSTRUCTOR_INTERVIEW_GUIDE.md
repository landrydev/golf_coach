# Instructor Interview Guide

**CURRENT DRAFT STATUS:** Revised for Business Plan V2  
**RESEARCH STATUS:** No interviews completed  
**Depends on:** [Research Plan](RESEARCH_PLAN.md), [Target Customer](../01_business_foundation/TARGET_CUSTOMER.md)

## Research purpose

Understand the actual assessment-to-package workflow, current follow-up, self-serve software behavior, seasonality, and buying conditions of individual golf instructors before exposing the proposed product.

The moderator must not use this interview to sell, onboard, or validate a predetermined answer.

## Target participant

An independent Canadian instructor or teaching professional who controls or influences package recommendations and can discuss a recent real assessment. Use the core/boundary classification in [TARGET_CUSTOMER.md](../01_business_foundation/TARGET_CUSTOMER.md).

## Session setup

- 55–65 minutes.
- Obtain consent for notes or recording under the approved research process.
- Ask the participant to bring a recent anonymized follow-up artifact if safe.
- Do not collect identifiable golfer records in this repository.
- Complete problem and workflow questions before showing any concept, sample, or price.
- Mark each answer as observed artifact, participant report, moderator inference, or concept reaction.

### Opening language

> We are studying how independent instructors move from an assessment or first lesson to an ongoing coaching recommendation. I am interested in what actually happened most recently, including things that did not work. This is not a sales call, and there are no right answers.

## Interview sequence

### 1. Instructor business and seasonal context — 7 minutes

- Tell me about your coaching business and how you control package decisions.
- What share of your work is one-off lessons versus multiple-session coaching?
- Walk me through your active months and off-season months in a typical year.
- Do you teach indoors or year-round? What changes by season?
- What software do you pay for personally? Which did you start without a call?
- Think of the last product you cancelled or paused. What happened to your data and return path?

Capture facility context but do not make facility ownership a qualification proxy.

### 2. Most recent assessment-to-decision case — 15 minutes

- Take me to the last assessment or first lesson where ongoing coaching was appropriate.
- What did the golfer want?
- What did you observe, record, or show?
- When did you decide what to recommend?
- How did you describe the sequence and first priority?
- What exactly did the golfer receive afterward?
- How did the package, price, and next action appear?
- What did the golfer do next, and how do you know?
- Where did uncertainty, delay, or extra explanation occur?

Ask to see the artifact or reconstruct the sequence. Avoid asking whether a hypothetical roadmap “would help” at this stage.

### 3. Contrast case — 8 minutes

- Describe a recent golfer who bought and one who did not.
- What differed before the decision?
- When does a golfer choose another single lesson instead of a package?
- Which part of your recommendation is hardest to communicate consistently?
- What current behavior would you not change?

### 4. Package and outcome baseline — 7 minutes

- What qualifies as a package in your business?
- What are typical package price, session count, and cadence ranges?
- How many eligible assessments occurred in a recent comparable period?
- How many led to packages, delayed choices, or no purchase?
- What records exist, and what is reconstructed from memory?
- Which outside factors change conversion: lead source, season, golfer goal, price, or coach availability?

Do not imply that the product can claim causality from a simple before/after comparison.

### 5. Ongoing value, renewal, and referral — 8 minutes

- What do golfers receive between lessons today?
- How do they know progress is occurring or the plan changed?
- What happens at a setback?
- How do you close a phase and recommend the next one?
- When is a referral request appropriate or inappropriate?
- What would make a separate roadmap product useful after the first package decision?

### 6. Workflow and administration — 7 minutes

- Which information already exists at assessment time?
- Where is it stored or communicated?
- What do you copy, rewrite, or assemble manually?
- What is the longest acceptable setup task before the first roadmap?
- What is the longest acceptable task per golfer?
- Which fields would you skip if optional?
- What kind of help would cause you to abandon a self-serve product?

### 7. Product discovery and buying behavior — 6 minutes

- How do you learn about coaching products?
- Which sources or peers do you trust?
- What must a website show before you create an account?
- Would a sample golfer roadmap be enough to judge fit? What else is needed?
- When do you prefer a trial, interactive demo, video, or call?
- Who besides you can block or approve purchase, if anyone?

## Concept-exposed section only

After the behavior interview, show the versioned landing message, realistic sample roadmap, self-serve flow summary, and transparent price presentation. Do not explain them first.

### Unaided comprehension

- What do you think this is?
- Who is it for?
- What would you need to provide?
- What does the golfer receive?
- How could this affect a package decision?
- What tools would it replace, if any?
- What would you expect to do immediately after signup?

Record exact retell before correcting misunderstandings.

### Fit and credibility

- Which part resembles your recent case, and which does not?
- What looks too generic or too polished for the stated effort?
- Which coaching judgment must remain yours?
- What would stop you before first share?
- Would you send this sample structure to a real golfer? Why or why not?

### Price and packaging

Show **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $75 per month per individual instructor** only in its actual candidate package context.

- What would you compare this fee with in your business?
- What specific result or repeated use would justify continuing?
- What would make you cancel after the first package opportunity?
- What included usage is clear or unclear?
- Would per-golfer pricing change your usage? How?
- Would a mandatory setup fee or annual contract change the decision?

Stated response is directional evidence only. Record actual paid behavior in later authorized stages.

### Seasonality

Present the candidate monthly cancel option and **[PRICING HYPOTHESIS — REQUIRES VALIDATION] CAD $15/month read-only seasonal pause** separately.

- What would you do in your quietest months?
- What must remain accessible?
- When and how would you expect to resume?
- Does a pause reduce friction or simply add another charge?
- Would annual billing help or deter you?

## Moderator evidence checklist

- Core or boundary segment recorded.
- Region and seasonal pattern recorded without unnecessary identifying detail.
- Recent behavior captured before concept reaction.
- Current artifacts and evidence limitations noted.
- Package and workflow baseline captured.
- Unaided concept retell preserved verbatim where consented.
- Support expectation and abandonment triggers recorded.
- Price response labelled stated, not paid.
- Seasonal cancel/pause/reactivation behavior explored.
- Contradictory evidence entered in [EVIDENCE_LOG.md](EVIDENCE_LOG.md).

## Closing

Ask what was missed, explain the status of the research truthfully, and do not promise access, results, a launch date, or a final price.
